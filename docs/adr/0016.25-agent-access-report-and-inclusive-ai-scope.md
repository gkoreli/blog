# ADR-0016.25: Agent access reports and inclusive AI scope

## Status

Accepted for implementation November 14, 2026. Extends [agent-mediated audience growth](0016.19-agent-mediated-audience-growth.md), [article reach](0016.18-article-reach-and-source-concentration.md) and [portable reporting](0016.24-portable-reporting-and-signature-domains.md). Source delivery, activation and production acceptance are recorded separately in the [engineering worklist](../worklist/tools-product-opportunities/agent-access-stats.md).

## Evidence and scope

Primary documents distinguish on-demand fetchers, search indexing and crawling: [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Anthropic](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/8896518) and [Perplexity](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers). These are service descriptions, not authentication of an observed request's purpose.

The November 14 source review checks [PostHog's role/type definitions at 91bf9ddb](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog/blob/91bf9ddb5d2dc9c35e9c2eea1e4f78b4d2f4ff14/products/web_analytics/backend/hogql_queries/bot_definitions.py) and [Rybbit's detection/evidence boundary at 840369a0](https://github.com/rybbit-io/rybbit/blob/840369a089c393ae16139c18ba1405efd14d32e6/server/src/services/tracker/botBlocking/index.ts). Adopt separate role, name and evidence dimensions; do not copy their detectors or treat every catalogue classification as authoritative. Robots control tokens also must not become invented authenticated HTTP identities.

[Cloudflare's native edge report](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/features/analyze-ai-traffic/) observes upstream attempts, status/transfer and requested/served formats. Our successful-page collector has a different population. The [native signed-agent boolean](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ruleset-engine/rules-language/fields/reference/cf.bot_management.signed_agent/) requires Enterprise Bot Management; missing fields remain unavailable. No new entitlement or collector is required here.

## Reporting domains

1. `agent-roles.ts` owns the public reporting scope. Agents includes stored `ai-assistant`, `ai-search`, `ai-crawler` and `signed-agent`. Other crawlers contains search-engine, SEO/site-audit and preview roles. Every stored kind still belongs to one disjoint group; missing/unrecognized kinds remain Unresolved. Preserve original reader kinds, rules and source bytes. The signed fallback remains explicitly historical or unknown-role.
2. `agent-access-contract.ts` owns catalogue identity, event accounting, completed-day metrics, evidence partitions and selection. The pure selection service unions active-day sets and never joins clients across days. Unknown names, representations and measurement stamps are closed states. Raw unmatched paths, unknown names and arbitrary publisher origins are omitted from public reports.
3. `agent-access-query.ts` adapts D1 and reuses the versioned referral, signature and network interpretations. Current projections are read only after cohort coverage checks. Missing/stale facts trigger a complete source-read fallback; no projection rebuild or historical rewrite is part of this feature.
4. `agent-access.ts` composes query, validation, metering, explicit admission choice and shared caching. The blog supplies its public resource catalogue and presentation. Article/client/role selection consumes a complete fetched report without per-row queries.

The catalogue declares current article, evidence and site ownership. Each path has one registered owner. Parent relationships are explicit. Current registry membership does not reconstruct historical availability. Retired prompt pages remain unregistered aggregates with no restored labels or links. A known file outside page collection is a coverage gap, not a zero-use claim.

## Report and invariants

Use 28 completed UTC days, four seven-day intervals, independently of the selected-period page-request report. Keep calculation time and scope visible. A canonical endpoint ignores caller-selected filter matrices. Cache identity includes the report version, client-catalogue profile, interpretation fingerprint, resource-catalogue digest, referral policy and UTC date; reports expire within an hour and at midnight.

Independent totals reconcile the original window, owner/client exclusions, referral exclusions, included agent and other roles, resource totals and role totals. Joint resource/client/role/format cells reconcile with measurement histories and signature/network partitions. Missing or contradictory results fail visibly. An unknown timestamp remains in event totals outside weekly rows. Active-day counts are unions and are not additive between rows.

Requests with a recorded signing outcome preserve its reviewed publisher independently of the declared name. Network membership remains a separate check and does not authenticate the client declaration. Reports interpret retained evidence; they do not reverify historical wire requests or establish people, training, citations or task completion.

## Native constraints and verification

[D1 documents](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/) limits of 100 bound parameters, 100 KB SQL and 32 function arguments. Bind compact catalogue/name arrays; return flat aggregates rather than oversized JSON-object calls. The native engine rejected the initial seven-section compound query despite Node SQLite acceptance. Separate counts/cells, measurement history and proof evidence into three statements in one transactional batch. This preserves one coherent database selection and avoids the expanded compound-query limit.

The initial native acceptance compares Node and actual Workerd/D1 reports over 48 synthetic originals, with 39 included agent events and 14 joint cells. It covers source reading, complete projection, missing/stale fallback and exact original-row preservation. Reported reads/writes and missing attempt metadata remain explicit. Initial harness constraint and local-listener failures are separate from accepted checks. Broader suites, scale controls, actual UI checks and bounded production acceptance remain engineering gates.

No schema migration, collector-field expansion, new identity, prompt retention or original-observation mutation is needed. Requested preference, content revision, upstream failures, new file access and answer-use trials remain separate future work.
