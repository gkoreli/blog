Goga wants active pain and unresolved tension protected when they are alive, but not as a rigid style or vulnerability quota. Articles may braid past-tense lessons and growth, present-tense pain or ambiguity, and future intent. A later continuation can share earned expertise about earlier work while staying relatable about new tensions alive then. Do not recommend waiting merely for expert hindsight; do not manufacture a wound when a retrospective, guide, decision record, or resolved lesson is the truer form.
