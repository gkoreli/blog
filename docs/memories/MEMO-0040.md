The publication exists to help Goga grow, help readers grow alongside him, and build in public by inviting a third-person eye into life and work. Valid subjects include pain and ambiguity, projects and architecture, methods and tenets, bets and plans, vision and attempts to change the world. Time is contextual: past tense reflects, analyzes, learns, and shares growth; present tense carries uncertainty, conditioning, judgment calls, calculated guesses, bets, and open wounds; future tense carries intention, possibility, and vision. Articles may braid all three. This is a purpose and truthfulness tenet, not a rigid style or vulnerability quota.
