# TASK-0067 — Adversarial review of article-discovery-positioning

## Verdict

The skill needs a truth gate before it offers optimization. Its invariant should be:

1. **Article truth:** `shape-article` supplies the living center, governing form, protected material, and honest ending.
2. **Doorway:** the new skill translates that existing truth into cold-reader/search handles. It may clarify; it may not enlarge the claim.
3. **Distribution:** a channel places the doorway before a possible reader. This is not discovery proof.
4. **Outcome:** impressions, clicks, reads, replies, corrections, subscriptions, citations, or other contact are separately observed events.

No arrow guarantees the next. A crawl is not selection; selection is not citation; citation is not referral; a pageview is not useful reader contact. The skill must never promise traffic. It can improve doorway fidelity, run bounded distribution, make an asset worth linking to, and learn from observed contact.

Severity: **S0** = reject publication until resolved; **S1** = reject the proposed positioning and recover before publication; **S2** = publish only as an explicitly bounded experiment, with no performance claim.

## Severity-ranked failure modes, rejection checks, recovery, and falsifiers

| Severity | Failure mode | Preflight rejection check | Recovery move | Evidence that would change the guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S0** | **False how-to or outcome promise** — an inquiry, essay, single build story, or partial experiment is packaged as a complete guide, best practice, proven method, or reproducible result. | Build a **doorway promise ledger** for H1, `seoTitle`, description, standfirst, headings, social hook, and distribution copy. Circle `how`, `guide`, `best`, `complete`, `proven`, `works`, imperatives, and outcome verbs. Reject if the body lacks the promised ordered method, prerequisites, tested context, failure modes, limits, or result. `How I…` may be a narrative but may not silently imply `how you can…`. | Narrow the doorway to what the body proves: field note, decision, failure, experiment, live question, or “what happened when I…”. Add instructions only when they already belong to the living center; do not turn the article into a tutorial to save the title. | Article-level reproduction across the stated envelope, with prerequisites, method, counterexamples, and failure conditions, can earn a bounded how-to promise. Search demand or CTR cannot. |
| **S0** | **Title/body mismatch** — the doorway promises a tool, comparison, benchmark, verdict, audience, time scope, or outcome the article does not deliver. | For every noun, comparison, number, scope word, and result in H1/`seoTitle`/description, point to body evidence. Require the first 100 words to orient the reader to the same artifact and story without revealing an unearned answer. Reject when `seoTitle` and H1 do not share substance, even if wording differs. Run the **swap test**: would the proposed title fit ten unrelated posts, or could another post wear it unchanged? | Remove unsupported handles; name the actual artifact, mechanism, moment, or tension. Preserve the literary H1 when appropriate and make the concrete `seoTitle` semantically equivalent, not a second thesis. | A materially changed body that now fulfills the promise, or article-specific reader/query evidence showing the previous interpretation was wrong. More impressions alone do not establish match. |
| **S0** | **Voice corruption / living-center flattening** — search language settles uncertainty, imports a generic lesson, removes status-costing material, or turns Goga’s arguing voice into documentation. | Freeze an **article passport** from `shape-article`: center, form, protected passages, unresolved boundary, and phrases closest to the arguing voice. Reject any positioning edit that changes them. Run the **doorway deletion test**: remove metadata/orientation/distribution copy; the body should still be the same article. For exposed essays, reject SEO-title gymnastics and agent-written emotional prose. | Revert body edits. Put only truthful concrete handles in metadata or a minimal orientation sentence. If no honest doorway exists, publish the plain doorway or ask the author for missing factual context; never fabricate stakes or feelings. | Only the author’s explicit change to the center, form, or language can change this guardrail. Ranking, traffic, citation, and conversion evidence cannot overrule voice ownership. |
| **S0** | **Keyword stuffing** — repeated variants, tool lists, tags, headings, or anchor text exist to manipulate ranking rather than help a reader. | Read the page aloud. Reject unnatural repetition, synonym piles, irrelevant tags, keyword lists, comparison-slug titles, or handles absent from the body. Ask of every occurrence: does it disambiguate a concept for a human? Do not use a keyword-density threshold; that creates a new superstition. | Keep the smallest stable vocabulary the article naturally uses. Remove query variants and irrelevant tags. Let one concrete noun do its job once; add a synonym only when readers genuinely use both and the distinction matters. | Verified reader language can justify a natural synonym or explicit alias. No search-volume number makes unnatural prose acceptable; an updated engine policy would not override the voice test. |
| **S0** | **Manufactured trend or freshness** — “everyone,” “the 2026 trend,” “exploding,” or “the future of” is invented to create urgency; the topic exists only because it trends. | Reject trend claims without a dated primary dataset, defined population, method, comparison period, and relevance to the article’s owned experience. Reject cosmetic date bumps and posts Goga would not otherwise want to write for his existing readers. | Replace the trend with the observed event and its boundary: “I saw…”, “in this repository…”, “among these N cases…”. Remove false urgency. Distribute to the people who actually share the problem. | A primary, time-stamped dataset with a defensible method plus an honest connection to the article can earn a bounded trend claim. A trend chart or search-volume spike alone cannot supply the article’s stake. |
| **S0** | **Backlink scheme / link exchange** — money, goods, reciprocal placement, partner pages, optimized anchors, or contractual links are used to manipulate ranking. | Reject quid pro quo links, `link to me and I’ll link to you`, automated placements, required optimized anchor text, unqualified sponsored links, and “partner” pages whose only reader value is cross-linking. | Remove the exchange requirement. Qualify legitimate paid/sponsored relationships as required. Earn links with a sourceable asset: original data, reproduction, code, decision table, failure taxonomy, diagram, or precise claim with provenance. Outreach may show relevant work to a relevant person; it may not demand ranking credit. | An independent editorial link given without consideration, or a legitimate relationship transparently qualified, changes the case. A ranking lift does not legitimize manipulation. |
| **S0** | **Duplicate SEO / doorway page** — substantially similar pages target query variants and funnel to the same useful article. | Compare center, reader job, evidence, decision, and outcome. Reject when only the keyword, region, tool order, or title changes. Reject a companion page that is less useful than the destination. Tags alone do not make a new page. | Consolidate into the canonical article and make its doorway clearer. Create a continuation only when changed evidence, a distinct reader job, a correction, a deeper implementation, or a time-separated state gives it an independent center. Use series metadata and one contextual prose link for real continuity. | Independent evidence, a distinct job-to-be-done, and a self-sufficient result can justify a separate page. Search volume for a variant cannot. |
| **S0** | **AEO/GEO folklore stated as fact** — FAQ schema, `llms.txt`, Markdown, “answer-first” prose, citations, or a crawler request is sold as guaranteed discovery, ranking, citation, or traffic. | Require every claim to name its stage: **eligibility → crawl/request → retrieval/selection → navigation → use in answer → citation → referral → useful contact**. Reject skipped stages, “AI-optimized” guarantees, fake-precise uplift, and any claim that a supplied-source/frozen-retrieval study proves organic acquisition. Reject FAQ boxes added only for schema. | Restate as a bounded hypothesis at the stage evidence supports. Keep evidence, clear claims, qualifications, crawlability, internal links, and accurate structured data because they help humans and eligibility—not as a traffic guarantee. Measure the intended stage separately. | Updated direct engine documentation, or replicated independent experiments that observe organic retrieval, citation, and referral with controls, could change a tactic. Crawler logs, smaller token count, or an answer produced from sources supplied by the experiment cannot. |
| **S0** | **Distribution or traffic guarantee** — a title, cross-post, aggregator submission, or social hook is represented as reader acquisition. | Reject forecasts phrased as outcomes: “will rank,” “will get cited,” “will drive traffic,” “will go viral.” Require channel, audience fit, action, cost, attribution method, and stop condition. | Phrase the plan as an action: publish canonically, cross-post with canonical URL, send to a named relevant community, or ask a specific practitioner. Report actual contact later. | A repeated, attributable channel history can support a probability range for that channel and article class, never a guarantee for an individual article. |
| **S1** | **Generic broadness** — a broad-growth piece becomes generic “AI agents,” “productivity,” or “context engineering” content that could have been written without Goga’s system or experience. | Run the **ten-blog test**: if ten competent blogs could publish the title, description, and first 200 words unchanged, reject. Require at least one owned artifact/mechanism, bounded reader problem, specific scene/evidence, and the article’s actual stake. Broad audience is allowed; generic evidence is not. | Widen the doorway through a recognizable reader problem while keeping the body concrete. Name the tool, mechanism, failure, decision, or strange result. If the piece is intentionally narrow identity/vision work, do not pretend it is a mass-intent guide. | Cold readers from the intended audience can accurately predict the article and report the intended value while the specific center remains intact. Large raw reach without fit does not. |
| **S1** | **Search-volume worship** — volume or difficulty chooses the topic, inflates scope, or overrides the living center. | Reject when keyword research occurs before content-to-intent mapping, when a high-volume term absent from the article is inserted, or when Goga lacks an owned stake/experience in the selected niche. Ask: would this article still deserve to exist at zero search volume? | Start from the article passport; use query language only to discover honest reader wording and doorway variants. Declare the article role—broad reader-growth, narrow identity/thinking/vision, or contextual hybrid—and judge it by that role. | Page-filtered queries showing good-fit impressions plus attributable useful contact may justify a doorway experiment. Search volume by itself never proves fit, value, or expected traffic. |
| **S1** | **Rigid portfolio ratio** — a permanent percentage of broad/narrow/hybrid posts forces material into the wrong role or treats narrow identity work as underperformance. | Reject universal quotas such as “60/30/10,” automatic alternation, or retrospective relabeling to satisfy a dashboard. Reject any ratio that changes a living center or delays a live piece. | Label each article’s role and expected value separately. Use a descriptive portfolio map and a time-bounded editorial question, not a prescriptive mix. Broad reach, narrow identity, and hybrids are all valid. | A declared, time-bounded campaign with a concrete constraint, enough articles, and outcomes tied to its goal may justify a temporary allocation. It expires with the campaign and never governs article form. |
| **S1** | **Pageview-driven editorial decision** — a spike, dip, or total views causes topic/body/voice changes without diagnosing who arrived or what happened. | Reject any editorial change whose evidence is only pageviews, impressions, average position, or one viral referral. Require page-filtered queries/referrers, a measurement window, audience fit, and a desired contact outcome. Separate content correction, doorway change, distribution change, new experiment, and no action. | Preserve the article. Fix factual errors immediately; otherwise wait for the declared window and diagnose. Prefer replies, corrections, relevant citations, subscribers, implementer reports, or attributable referrals. Use pageviews as reach context, not an editorial command. | If the publication’s declared business model becomes impression-based, or pageviews can be attributed to the desired readers and correlated with the desired contact/conversion, they can inform distribution. They still cannot override truth or voice. |
| **S1** | **Internal-link / related-post spam** — links are added because tags overlap or because internal links are presumed to rank. | Reject footer link piles, repeated links to the same relationship, optimized anchors that do not read naturally, and links justified only by shared tags. Require predecessor, sequel, correction, deeper dive, or product-boundary relationship. | Use `series` for a deliberate sequence and one root-relative prose link where it helps the reader. Add a forward link to the older post when the new post materially continues it. | Reader navigation data plus observed confusion may reveal a missing relationship, but every added link still needs a semantic reason. |

## Mandatory preflight rejection protocol

Run this after `shape-article` and the governing form skill, before publication or distribution. The new skill does not choose the living center.

1. **Freeze the article passport.** Record living center, form, protected passages, evidence boundary, honest ending, article role, intended reader, and the reader job the body can actually serve. Missing human stake goes back to the author; it is not generated.
2. **Inventory every doorway.** H1, `seoTitle`, alternative headline, description, standfirst/orientation sentence, headings, tags, slug, social hooks, cross-post title, internal anchors, and outreach copy.
3. **Run claim entailment.** For every promise, mark exact body evidence and limit. Any unsupported noun, number, comparison, superlative, how-to, trend, or outcome is an S0 rejection.
4. **Run semantic-equivalence and cold-reader tests.** H1 may be literary and `seoTitle` concrete, but both must identify the same article. From the doorway alone, write the three things a cold reader would expect; the body must deliver all three.
5. **Run center invariance and voice tests.** Compare against protected material. Delete the doorway layer mentally: no body claim, form, tense, resolution, or emotional language may have changed merely for acquisition.
6. **Run naturalness and specificity tests.** Read aloud; reject query variants, generic topic language, or terms absent from the article. Pass the ten-blog test with owned artifacts, mechanisms, evidence, and stakes.
7. **Run uniqueness and relationship tests.** Compare with existing posts by center, reader job, evidence, and outcome. Consolidate duplicate doorway pages. Add internal links only for a real series/continuation/correction/deep-dive relationship.
8. **Run evidence/freshness tests.** Trend, performance, and comparison claims need source, date, method, boundary, counterevidence, and what would disprove them. No cosmetic freshness.
9. **Run link-integrity tests.** Record consideration, relationship, anchor choice, and reader value for every planned earned/paid/reciprocal placement. S0 on ranking-credit exchanges.
10. **Run stage accounting.** Every search/AEO/GEO claim and metric is assigned to one stage from eligibility through useful contact. If the proposed inference jumps a stage, reject it.
11. **Run role-fit and distribution tests.** State broad, narrow, or hybrid role without a quota. State distribution as channel actions with audience fit and attribution—not promised traffic.
12. **Set the learning window before publishing.** Name signals and the decision they can trigger: correction, doorway change, distribution change, experiment, or no action. Pageviews alone trigger none.

**Release rule:** all S0 and S1 failures block the proposed positioning. S2 experiments must name hypothesis, stage, baseline, window, attribution limits, stop condition, and evidence that would reverse the decision. An exception may not be justified by “SEO best practice,” traffic desire, or a tool score.

## Evidence base

- **Google Search spam policies:** defines doorway abuse as similar query-targeted pages that funnel users, keyword stuffing as unnatural/out-of-context repetition, and link spam to include buying/selling ranking links and excessive exchanges. This directly supports S0 treatment of doorway pages, stuffing, and exchanges.
- **Google title-link guidance** (last updated 2025-12-10): asks for descriptive, concise, distinct titles; warns against stuffing and boilerplate; and says inaccurate titles may be replaced using H1, prominent text, anchors, and other sources. This supports semantic match, not identical H1/`seoTitle` wording.
- **Google people-first content guidance** (last updated 2025-12-10): flags content made primarily for search visits, entering niches for traffic, writing because a topic trends, promising answers that do not exist, and cosmetic freshness. It explicitly permits SEO when applied to people-first content.
- **Google AI-feature guidance** (last updated 2025-12-10): says AI Overviews/Mode have no additional technical requirements and require no specific optimization beyond existing SEO fundamentals; it recommends crawlability, internal links, textual content, and structured data that matches visible text. This rejects “special AEO hack” claims.
- **Aggarwal et al., “GEO: Generative Engine Optimization,” KDD 2024:** the main setup fetches only Google’s top five sources before generation; therefore its reported visibility improvements concern use/presentation after a bounded retrieval setup, not universal organic discovery or referral. Effects vary by domain.
- **Julia Evans, “Measuring a blog’s success: focus on conversations, not page views” (2018-02-20):** distinguishes widely read from helpful and values conversations, corrections, sustained readers, and reports of usefulness. This is practitioner evidence, not a universal causal study, but it matches this publication’s explicit contact goal.
- **Current project rules and skills:** `shape-article` owns form/living center; `personal-essays` owns voice and exposed-register boundaries; `blog-writing` owns structure/sourcing; `shareable-engineering` already separates GEO stages and treats contact—not pageviews—as success; `AGENTS.md` requires query diagnosis before content changes and continuity links only for predecessor/sequel/correction/deeper-dive/product-boundary relationships.

## Unknowns and limits

- No reliable cross-engine attribution currently proves that a page was organically retrieved, used, cited, and then referred; stage-specific evidence remains necessary.
- No article-specific query, referral, reply, correction, subscription, or citation data was supplied for this review. Thresholds must not be invented.
- The useful boundary between a distinct continuation and a duplicate doorway page is semantic; word-overlap or similarity scores cannot decide it alone.
- There is no evidence for an optimal broad/narrow/hybrid portfolio ratio. The portfolio map should remain descriptive until a bounded goal and sufficient longitudinal evidence exist.
- Title length/word-count ranges are probabilistic presentation heuristics, not rejection gates. Accuracy and semantic match outrank them.
- Google guidance is platform-specific and mutable. Re-audit dated platform rules; do not generalize one engine’s documentation to all discovery systems.

## Concrete implications for the new skill

1. Require an input contract from `shape-article`: article passport, not a topic brief.
2. Make the first output a content-to-intent map and doorway promise ledger; keyword/search-volume tooling may run only afterward.
3. Include the severity gates above as a mandatory **Positioning Preflight**. Bad positioning is rejected, not merely scored.
4. Keep H1/body/voice under their governing skills. Own only truthful translation into `seoTitle`, descriptions, headings where natural, tags/keywords as labels, cross-references, link-worthy assets, and distribution plans.
5. Require a declared article role—broad reader-growth, narrow identity/thinking/vision, or hybrid—with role-specific expectations and no ratio.
6. Separate doorway optimization, distribution, link earning, and actual reader contact in every recommendation and report.
7. Ban guaranteed acquisition language and require stage labels for all SEO/AEO/GEO claims.
8. Require substantive uniqueness before proposing a new page; prefer canonical consolidation and series/contextual links.
9. Optimize for link-worthiness through owned evidence and useful artifacts, never exchange mechanics.
10. Define a pre-publication measurement window and decision taxonomy. Pageviews and impressions are diagnostic context; useful contact and article-specific evidence govern learning.
