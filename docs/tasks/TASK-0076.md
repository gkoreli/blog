# Decision

Create standalone `article-discovery-positioning` after a clean ownership cutover. The skill runs after shape/governing form and before shareable-engineering. It translates the article that exists into truthful cold-reader doorways; it never chooses or flattens the living center.

# Publication portfolio

Reader-growth, signature, and bridge articles are all first-class. Roles are descriptive, contextual, and quota-free. Broad reach is not a quality ranking; narrow voice/vision is not a discovery failure. Metadata and link graph can help the roles reinforce each other without making every article do both jobs. AGENTS.md now records the model and skill order; MEMO-0077 preserves it durably.

# Skill delivered

`.agents/skills/article-discovery-positioning/SKILL.md` v1.0.0 defines:

- authority, order, conflicts, and article-passport inputs;
- reader-growth/signature/bridge roles;
- content inventory and content-to-intent workflow;

- ethical distribution/link-earning boundary;
- separate doorway/distribution/backlink/contact outcomes;

- worked reader-growth, signature, and bridge examples.
