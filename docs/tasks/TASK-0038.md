# TASK-0032 — Transferable engineering principles and mental model

Reasoning artifact, not article prose. Sources: ADR-0016, ADR-0016.1, TASK-0012–TASK-0021, PROMPT-0001, current implementation, and primary metric/privacy references.

## Publication tenet and editorial boundary

The writing exists to help Goga grow, help others grow alongside him, and build in public by inviting a third-person eye into his life and work. Its subjects are not limited to pain or ambiguity: projects, architecture, methods, vision, tenets, bets, plans, and attempts to change the world also belong. Time is contextual: the past supports reflection, analysis, learning, and sharing; the present can hold an open wound, uncertainty, conditioning, calculated judgment, and bets; the future carries intention, vision, and possibility. Use whatever braid the material needs. This is a publication tenet, not a style template.

Publish the immediate article from the evidence and active work now; do not wait 30 days merely to sound retrospective or expert. “Open wound” is not a quota. Preserve tensions that are genuinely alive, but do not erase earned growth or manufacture exposure. The continuation may describe this cutover in the past tense while staying honest about whatever new wound, judgment, or possibility is alive then.

Current tensions worth protecting:

- The old counter worked, but the claim attached to it was wrong.
- The new boundary is more truthful, but still best effort, heuristic, HTML-only, and unable to prove reading or AI use.
- Retaining 2,564 rows preserves meaningful history, but joins two non-identical instruments.
- HMAC reduces an enumeration threat, but still processes IP/UA and creates within-day pseudonymous linkage.
- The rewrite was justified by structural semantic failure, but the corrected instrument could become infrastructure-as-procrastination.
- The 30-day result may strengthen, narrow, or disappoint today's theory; it is a sequel, not a prerequisite for publishing the owned architecture and mistake.

## Mental model

```text
real occurrence -> observable event -> eligibility predicate -> recorded row
                -> aggregate -> public word -> decision
```

A counter can be arithmetically correct while the product claim is false. Trust fails when a later stage promises more than an earlier stage observed. The old system observed a best-effort browser beacon POST, then called aggregates `Views`, `Visitors`, and `AI Reads`. Repair therefore starts with the event, not the query or dashboard.

Evidence states below: **Observed** = production/reproduction; **Code-inspected** = cited implementation; **Decision** = accepted ADR/owner choice; **Inference** = derived principle; **Unknown** = not established.

## 1. Name the event before naming the metric

**Principle.** Define the smallest observable fact with method, success condition, media type, exclusions, time, and failure semantics. Only then choose a public noun.

ADR-0016 defines `PageObservation` as one successful, non-prefetch `GET` whose served response is HTML, excluding APIs and `/stats`. It proves a served eligible response. It does not prove rendering, attention, reading, comprehension, approval, indexing, citation, training, or model use.

**Evidence.** The old static page bypassed the Worker and later emitted `POST /api/event`; non-JS crawlers could fetch an article without a D1 row (TASK-0012; old `pageShell` and `handleEvent` at pinned commit `c85a629…`). The new path is `packages/blog/src/worker/index.ts:fetch` -> `env.ASSETS.fetch` -> `packages/analytics/src/index.ts:observePageResponse`, guarded by `eligibility.ts:isEligiblePageResponse`. TASK-0019 reproduced Browser and JavaScript-disabled GPTBot HTML observations while `/stats` remained excluded.

**Decision rules.**
1. List the next unobserved stages: served -> rendered -> attended -> read -> understood -> acted on. Never skip a stage in the label.
2. Use a noun that remains true under blocked JS, spoofed headers, dropped writes, retries, and cache lag.
3. If the desired question lies outside the predicate, define another event or admit the gap.
4. Zero means zero recorded eligible events, not proof that the real occurrence did not happen.

**Examples.**
1. A 200 HTML `GET` with `GPTBot/1.0` can yield one “recorded HTML response matching the GPTBot UA rule,” never “GPT read the article.”
2. `GET /article.md` and `GET /llms.txt` are non-HTML and produce no `PageObservation`. If known-site agent navigation becomes decision-relevant, it needs a separate resource-request metric.

**Anti-patterns.** Relabeling a beacon as a request; naming a UA match after an outcome (`AI Reads`); defining events from cards backward; treating response, background write, and cached aggregate as atomic; rescuing a false headline metric with a footnote.

**Limits.** A browser beacon is correct for browser execution/RUM questions if named honestly. Best-effort counts suit directional publishing decisions, not billing or safety ledgers. “Page view” is acceptable only with a published method boundary.

## 2. Observable facts versus product words

**Principle.** Public vocabulary is part of the data contract. Product words must not add identity, intent, or cognition.

```text
IP + UA HMAC -> daily client ID -> estimated daily client
UA string -> matched rule -> heuristic traffic class
Referer host -> attributable referral -> possible channel
HTML response -> page observation -> page-view count under stated method
```

Every arrow loses certainty. Words must weaken, not strengthen.

**Evidence.** `packages/analytics/src/contracts.ts:StatsResponse` exposes views and daily clients, not people/readers. `packages/blog/src/pages/stats.ts:statsPage` uses Page views, Daily clients, Browsers, Bots, AI UAs, and Matched User-Agent rules. TASK-0017 caught remaining semantic drift in “Named automation agents,” hourly Daily-client units, and human-verification copy, proving that integrity must reach cards, tables, ARIA, and privacy text.

**Decision rules.** Actor nouns require identity; cognitive verbs require cognition; uniqueness must be scoped in the noun; missing referrer is unattributed, not necessarily direct; units belong at every point of use.

**Examples.**
1. One person over two UTC days becomes two daily IDs; two people behind one IP with the same UA may collapse. “Daily clients” is bounded; “visitors” is not.
2. Six `chatgpt.com` referrers establish attributable browser arrivals from that host, not crawler access, model use, or citation. AI referral and AI-UA request are separate facts.

**Anti-patterns.** Unmatched UA = human; rule name = verified agent; hourly daily IDs summed as additive; traffic volume = quality/engagement/audience.

**Limits.** Product copy need not mirror SQL columns. Authenticated accounts or cryptographic bot identity can support stronger nouns than this system can.

## 3. Bounded contexts follow questions and reasons to change

**Principle.** A bounded context owns vocabulary/policy; a composition root owns wiring. Split meanings, not merely files.

- Blog Worker: HTTP routing and asset delivery.
- Analytics package: eligibility, minimization, classification, daily identity, persistence, windows, aggregates.
- Stats client: URL state and public presentation.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics/client error reports: separate browser-performance and diagnostic questions.

**Evidence.** ADR-0016 domain diagram; `worker/index.ts:fetch`; `analytics/index.ts:persistObservation`. TASK-0018 found analytics `Env` wrongly owned `ASSETS` and `handleStats` had broader dependencies than used. Integration narrowed them and added the browser-safe shared `contracts.ts` subpath.

**Decision rules.** Ask who owns the meaning; keep sequencing in one root; pass narrow dependencies; share stable contracts; split edge response, RUM timing, search click, and reader contact because their evidence differs.

**Examples.**
1. Eligibility needs Request plus served Response, so Worker fetches first; analytics owns the predicate, not `ASSETS`.
2. Stats needs D1/reporting policy, not hash secret or asset fetcher. Narrow authority makes the boundary visible.

**Anti-patterns.** God `AnalyticsService`; one broad `Env` everywhere; one “AI traffic” context for requests/referrals/indexing/citations; package-per-helper fragmentation.

**Limits.** Small systems need few physical packages. If policies always change together and share invariants, separating them may hide the real aggregate. Composition roots legitimately know many dependencies.

## 4. Composition over inheritance—because policies vary independently

**Principle.** Composition is evidence-led here, not ideology. Eligibility, classification, identity, persistence, aggregation, and presentation have no honest subtype relation.

**Evidence.** `observePageResponse -> isEligiblePageResponse -> persistObservation -> extractRequestMetadata/classifyTraffic/createDailyClientId -> recordPageObservation` in `packages/analytics/src/index.ts`. Reporting separately composes window construction, half-open SQL predicates, batched aggregates, and bucket completion in `stats.ts`/`dates.ts`. TASK-0018 confirmed this architecture while exposing the need for composed integration tests.

**Decision rules.** Compose independent change axes; isolate D1/clock/crypto/Worker seams; test the composed contract, not helpers alone; introduce interfaces after a second real variation.

**Examples.**
1. Adding an AI UA rule changes classification, not eligibility/HMAC/repository; an `AiPageObserver` subclass would bind unrelated concerns.
2. Hourly versus daily reporting changes window, bucket, and UI unit—not ingestion.

**Anti-patterns.** Browser/bot/AI subclasses; abstract repository with one backend; helper tests presented as end-to-end proof; callback-heavy “composition framework.”

**Limits.** Inheritance remains valid for real substitutable hierarchies. Composition can permit invalid combinations; constrain them with types or one constructor. One concrete implementation often beats injectable interfaces everywhere.

## 5. Source-aware migrations preserve history without inventing facts

**Principle.** Measurement migrations change row meaning. Preserve provenance as data, retain a lossless archive, build a minimized read model, make copying idempotent, and keep unknowns unknown.

**Evidence.** Production held exactly 2,564 valid legacy rows, March 7–August 26, across 39 paths, 32 referrers, and 69 countries. TASK-0020 proved source 2,564, copied 2,564, missing 0. `migrations/0002_backfill_legacy_page_views.sql` adds `observation_source`, retains `source_event_id`, creates a unique replay guard, minimizes referrer to host, maps classes, and uses `INSERT OR IGNORE`. Historical `agent_name` remains null because raw UA was never stored. The 16-hex ID is duplicated to 32 characters only to preserve equality under the new schema shape—not to claim stronger cryptography.

**Decision rules.** Separate archive/read model; add source and method-change date before combining; transform only derivable facts; leave unknown fields null; prove conservation/idempotency; state which analyses survive.

**Examples.**
1. Preserve the April spike and long-term page demand but mark those rows `beacon`; disclose that edge coverage starts August 26.
2. Preserve ID equality through representation migration, while refusing to infer historical agent rules.

**Anti-patterns.** Delete imperfect history; combine eras without source; reconstruct missing facts; keep old ingestion “for compatibility”; accept “SQL ran” without row proof.

**Limits.** Split/discard when units are incompatible, provenance absent, privacy forbids retention, or high-stakes decisions require strict comparability. Directional blog analytics is not a precedent for finance or safety data.

## 6. When correctness may yield to useful continuity

**Principle.** Correctness has dimensions. The decision did not waive correctness; it let single-era semantic purity yield to useful, disclosed continuity.

**Non-negotiable:** truthful new event/names; inspectable source; no invented fields; coherent owner/aggregate predicates; no privacy weakening.

**Negotiable:** broad trends across two methods; approximate daily-client estimates with limits; best-effort editorial counts; manual snapshots instead of premature range tooling.

**Rule.** Keep continuity only if legacy facts still answer a bounded question, lineage survives, transformation invents nothing, disclosure is adjacent, exact equivalence is unnecessary, and historical value exceeds misuse risk.

**Examples.**
1. Keep 877 events / 615 daily IDs for the historical leading article as evidence of a distribution event; do not treat pre/post AI-UA totals as one crawler-growth series.
2. Combined All-time answers “which pages ever attracted demand?” Source-aware post-cutover data answers “which declared automation requested HTML after cutover?” The question chooses the boundary.

**Anti-patterns.** “Perfect is the enemy of good” used to waive provenance; “data is better than no data” used for an irrelevant event; clean cutover = delete history; continuity = retain obsolete ingestion.

## 7. Privacy is a threat model, not an absence claim

**Principle.** “No cookies” is not a privacy model. State the purpose, attacker, exposure, linkage window, retained fields, and deletion policy. Minimize at ingestion; call pseudonymisation what it is.

**Evidence.** `hash.ts:createDailyClientId` derives a daily HMAC key, length-prefixes fields, signs site/date/IP/UA with HMAC-SHA-256, and returns 128 bits. `metadata.ts:extractRequestMetadata` keeps pathname and external referrer host. `db.ts:recordPageObservation` persists no raw IP/UA. `pages/privacy.ts:privacyPage` discloses transient processing, within-day linkage, cross-day/device/network limits, retained fields, and no automatic analytics expiry. TASK-0013, RFC 2104, EDPB, and CNIL establish that public-date SHA is enumerable and pseudonymisation is not anonymisation.

**Decision rules.** Link only for a named decision; prefer aggregate/entrance when identity is unnecessary; use site/day-scoped required HMAC with structured inputs and fail closed; persist only used derivatives; acknowledge transient processing; define retention; exclude owner traffic through server-visible controls.

**Examples.**
1. SHA-256(public date + IP + UA) rotates equality but remains candidate-enumerable. Secret HMAC changes the leak threat; it does not erase within-day linkage.
2. `referrer_host = reddit.com` answers a channel question. Full path/query may expose terms/tokens without helping the decision.

**Anti-patterns.** Cookieless = anonymous; unkeyed hash = safe; optional secret with fallback; collect raw fields “for later”; privacy text narrower than schema; cross-day identity for a familiar dashboard card.

**Limits.** Authenticated identity/fraud/support linkage can be legitimate with explicit purpose/access/retention. HMAC is not automatic anonymisation. Small public dimensions may still disclose; no universal safe threshold is proven. Current indefinite retention is a disclosed limitation, not a model to copy.

## 8. Separate edge analytics, non-HTML requests, RUM, and reader contact

**Principle.** Instrument where the fact exists. These are complementary instruments, not interchangeable analytics.

**Evidence.** ADR-0016.1: edge establishes eligible HTML response, path, time, referrer host, coarse geography, UA class. Browser RUM establishes LCP/INP/CLS, resource timing, failures, interactions. `pages/privacy.ts` names Cloudflare Web Analytics and client diagnostics as separate; neither feeds D1 stats. `eligibility.ts` excludes Markdown, `llms.txt`, feeds, assets, and all non-HTML responses.

**Decision rules.** Locate the fact first; never infer browser performance from edge response; never infer non-HTML navigation from HTML; never merge datasets without units/source/decision; build custom RUM only when existing tools cannot answer a recurring performance decision.

**Examples.**
1. Successful HTML can coexist with slow LCP or JS failure. Edge records the first; RUM/error reporting records the second.
2. An agent can fetch `/llms.txt` then `/article.md` with no HTML. Current D1 records neither. That is an observation gap, not zero agent use.

**Anti-patterns.** Edge count = engagement; standard scroll/session metrics without a decision; HTML counts used for Markdown/`llms.txt`; Cloudflare WA and D1 combined without definition reconciliation.

**Limits.** One vendor may house edge and RUM if every metric preserves source/unit. If non-HTML telemetry becomes useful, add a separate metric: one HTML view plus three resource fetches is not four page views.

## 9. Decision-driven instrumentation and the stop condition

**Principle.** The writing is the product. Every metric must support content correction, discoverability change, distribution change, bounded experiment, or no action. Strong contact outranks context counters.

**Evidence.** ADR-0016.1 ranks replies, corrections, subscriptions, citations, links, and reproduced use above page totals. It sets default windows: 24h deployment health; 7d launch; 30d near-term demand; 90d/All durability; 28d after confirmed recrawl for search doorway changes. Engineering requires a recurring unanswered decision, precise event, named action, explicit privacy/retention/public wording, and lower maintenance cost than manual analysis.

**Decision rules.** Name action first; declare window before results; save baseline; make one attributable change; read stronger signals first; choose exactly one outcome including no action; preserve evidence; productize only after manual work recurs.

**Examples.**
1. For a search-title change, confirm recrawl, then evaluate the predeclared 28-day window with page-filtered queries; do not churn metadata from daily views.
2. A 7-day Reddit spike supports a channel conclusion, not a quality claim or instruction to imitate the subject.

**Anti-patterns.** Dashboard-driven sessions/funnels/ranges; favorable window chosen after spike; five simultaneous interventions with causal claims; no-action treated as failure; pageviews substituted for contact.

**Limits.** React inside 24h to broken writes/routing/partitions/privacy. Exploratory analysis may precede a decision but should remain a query until repeated. A small public dashboard can have a transparency purpose even when every card does not trigger an edit.

## 10. When the rebuild effort is justified

**Principle.** Rewrite infrastructure when the observation boundary cannot answer the owned recurring question and the mismatch causes misleading claims or decisions—not merely because cleaner code is possible.

**Why this crossed the threshold:** relabeling could not make a beacon observe non-JS crawlers; `AI Reads`/`Visitors` overstated the fact; UTC identity and viewer-local buckets conflicted; populations/windows were inconsistent; privacy copy omitted fields; the intended HTML/automation question lived at the edge; history could be retained with lineage.

**Rule.** Rewrite when labels cannot repair the event, the new locus observes the needed fact, privacy improves or is explicit, migration/cutover boundaries are defined, and inclusions/exclusions can be reproduced. Otherwise relabel, query manually, or choose no action.

**Counterexamples.** An arbitrary-range picker is not justified while saved snapshots suffice. Custom RUM is not justified while Cloudflare WA, error reports, and focused reproductions answer current decisions.

## Evidence ledger

### Observations
- Old D1 rows were browser beacon events, not original page requests.
- Old public words exceeded the stored fact.
- New Worker observes successful non-prefetch HTML and schedules best-effort persistence.
- Production retained and source-marked all 2,564 legacy rows; archive remains intact.
- Dashboard discloses the August 26 method change.
- Edge daily IDs use required secret HMAC; raw IP/UA are not retained in edge rows.
- D1 stats, Cloudflare WA, and client diagnostics remain separate.

### Inferences
- Owned center: “a correct counter for the wrong event produced misleading claims,” not “server analytics is always better.”
- Reusable repair order: event -> vocabulary -> contexts -> privacy -> aggregates -> presentation -> decision loop.
- Source lineage can preserve history without pretending equivalence.
- Composition fit independent policies; it is not an anti-class rule.
- Decision-driven instrumentation supplies the stop rule.

### Unknowns/limits
- No clean 30-day post-cutover outcome exists yet. Publish current evidence now; sequel may strengthen, narrow, or disappoint the theory.
- HTML-only observation cannot establish Markdown/`llms.txt` use.
- UA matches cannot establish provider identity, indexing, citation, training, or model use.
- Browser class cannot establish human.
- Best-effort D1 drop/error rate is unknown without reconciliation.
- Historical owner exclusion cannot produce an exact external-reader count.
- Combined history cannot support exact cross-era comparison for every class.
- First-party analytics has no automatic expiry.
- Privacy findings are engineering threat-model conclusions, not legal advice.

## Article implications

1. Publish now; do not require a 30-day result. But do not force an “open-wound” shape: braid earned past lessons, true current tension, and future intent as the material requires.
2. Lead from the wrong event: “The counter was correct. The claim was not.”
3. Trace beacon boundary -> misleading words; reject a generic client-vs-server tutorial.
4. Compare architectures by ownership/event semantics, not file counts.
5. Preserve the production reversal: initial pure series, then 2,564-row source-aware continuity. Present it as thinking in motion, not a universal verdict.
6. Explain HMAC through its threat model and remaining pseudonymous linkage.
7. State the HTML/non-HTML gap plainly.
8. Keep RUM and direct reader contact separate; stronger contact evidence outranks counts.
9. Retain real uncertainties—best effort, spoofable UA, cross-era limits, retention, unknown 30-day effect—without manufacturing vulnerability or erasing earned expertise.
10. Keep novelty distinct from `does-llms-txt-work`: this is about event semantics, migration lineage, privacy, composition, and instrumentation discipline.
