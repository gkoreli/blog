# Assigned task

TASK-0063 — Audit discovery skill boundaries.

# Decision

Create `article-discovery-positioning` as a standalone skill, but only as a clean ownership cutover, not as a sixth overlapping editorial checklist. It has a distinct automatic-routing moment: after an article's substance and form are stable, or when a published article has evidenced doorway mismatch, translate the article that exists into honest ways outsiders can recognize and reach it.

A reference inside `shareable-engineering` would fail to route personal, inquiry, field-note, and vision pieces. A reference inside `shape-article` would mix external positioning into the decision about what the article is. A `blog-writing` section would conflate publication mechanics with content-to-intent judgment. The workflow is recurring across publication and post-publication diagnosis, and its inputs/outputs are separable, satisfying the AGENTS rule that a standalone skill needs repeated use and distinct automatic routing.

The skill must permit three equally legitimate portfolio roles:

- **Broad reader-growth:** the content already answers a recognizable, wider reader problem.
- **Narrow identity/thinking/vision:** the value is precise recognition, continuity, or contact among a smaller right audience.
- **Contextual hybrid:** one concrete doorway leads into a piece whose deeper value is voice, worldview, or project vision.

It must never infer that broad reach is the default or that traffic is the result it can guarantee.

# Boundary matrix

| Concern | Normative owner | `article-discovery-positioning` role | Boundary / conflict rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Living center, live contradiction, protected passages | `shape-article` | Consume and restate as a frozen constraint | Never choose, replace, settle, or broaden the center |
| Governing form and honest ending | `shape-article` | Position the chosen form for outsiders | Intent cannot turn inquiry into verdict, field note into guide, or exposed essay into lesson |
| Voice, status cost, human spine, exposed-register prose | `personal-essays` | Find truthful external handles already present | `personal-essays` wins; no manufactured universality, claim, feeling, CTA, or `seoTitle` gymnastics |
| Body structure, frontmatter syntax, formatting, sourcing | `blog-writing` | Supply semantic doorway choices and body-change requests | `blog-writing` owns valid form and sources; discovery cannot insert unsourced context or impose generic article structure |
| Engineering trust/share mechanics | `shareable-engineering` | Use the resulting specificity, evidence, dead ends, and reader value when mapping intent | It remains the engineering-content audit; its evidence informs positioning but it no longer independently owns doorway selection |
| Search/title/GEO evidence bounds | One shared evidence reference extracted from or linked to `shareable-engineering` | Apply those bounds to the actual article | Do not copy statistics, citations, or crawler claims into the new SKILL body |
| H1/title meaning | New skill proposes; governing voice skill approves | Choose the reader-facing doorway that tells the truth about the article | Literary titles remain valid; shape/personal constraints beat cold-reader optimization |
| `seoTitle`, description, `alternativeHeadline` semantics | New skill | Decide whether fields are needed and write content-derived values | AGENTS field semantics are hard constraints; no comparison slugs, keyword stuffing, or derived `alternativeHeadline` |
| Tags/keywords | New skill chooses semantic handles; `blog-writing`/schema governs format | Select 2–5 honest labels from entities, topic, artifact, or practice | No invented search volume; JSON-LD keywords continue to derive from tags |
| Orientation sentence, standfirst, headings | New skill identifies doorway need; shape/governing skill approves body expression | Propose minimal cold-reader anchors and navigational headings | Never reveal an answer the article lacks or add headings that violate its form; body changes return to the governing pass |
| Series and prose cross-links | AGENTS owns mechanics; new skill owns relationship selection | Audit predecessor/sequel/correction/deeper-dive/product-boundary relationships and propose exact links | Tags alone never justify a link; use both series trail and one contextual prose link when the relationship qualifies |
| Link-worthy assets | New skill identifies; governing/evidence skill validates | Inventory existing quotable/reusable artifacts and recommend only content-earned additions | No bolted-on FAQ, table, benchmark, checklist, or diagram merely to attract links |
| Distribution | New skill chooses audience/channel/hook; `blog-writing` owns canonical/cross-post mechanics | Match a specific doorway to a place and conversation where it belongs | Publish at gkoreli.com first; channel activity is not discovery, traffic, or contact proof |
| Measurement and iteration | AGENTS editorial learning loop | Define stage-specific evidence and a no-churn window | Separate doorway optimization, distribution, link earning, and actual reader contact; never promise traffic |
| Sentence polish | `polish-prose` | Provide stable meaning for the final prose pass | Runs last and may not smooth away voice, precision, or positioning semantics |

# Proposed trigger language

```yaml
name: article-discovery-positioning
description: Translate a shaped gkoreli.com article into honest publication doorways based on its actual content and portfolio role. Use after shape-article when choosing or reviewing reader/search intent, title and seoTitle, description, headings, tags/keywords, internal series and prose links, link-worthy assets, or distribution; also use when page-level evidence shows a published article has a doorway mismatch. Applies to broad-growth, narrow identity/voice/vision, and hybrid pieces. Never use it to choose the living center, rewrite an article around a keyword, or promise traffic.
```

Positive routing phrases include: “position this article,” “who is this for,” “search intent,” “title/seoTitle/description,” “make this discoverable,” “internal links/series,” “link-worthy,” “distribution plan,” and “good impressions but no clicks.” Do not route for topic ideation, form selection, ordinary drafting, source research, sentence polish, or generic site SEO infrastructure.

# Input contract

Required:

1. The current article body and metadata, or the served published article for a revision.
2. A `shape-article` result: living center, governing form, protected material, honest ending, and evidence boundary. If absent, run `shape-article`; do not reconstruct the center from imagined search demand.
3. The governing skill's stable draft or review result.
4. Publication context: existing post titles/slugs, tags, series, and direct predecessor/sequel/correction/deeper-dive/product-boundary candidates.
5. Mode: new publication or post-publication revision. For a revision, include page-filtered query/referral/contact evidence when available; AGENTS forbids changing content merely from aggregate impressions or intuition.

Optional but useful: the author's desired kind of contact, an explicitly chosen portfolio role, channel history, reader language from real replies, attributable referrals, and known communities. Missing optional data must become an unknown, not fabricated demand.

# Output contract

Produce one positioning brief with these fields:

1. **Frozen center:** verbatim/faithful center, form, protected material, and claims the doorway must not overstate.
2. **Portfolio role:** broad, narrow, or hybrid; why the content earns that role; what role it must not be forced to play.
3. **Reader/intention map:** primary right reader, the situation or job that makes the piece useful now, secondary reader if genuinely present, and explicit wrong-fit/excluded intents (for example docs/reference intent versus essay/opinion intent). Never assert demand volume without evidence.
4. **Doorway thesis:** one sentence connecting a concrete content handle to the article's true value without giving it a false thesis.
5. **Metadata package:** final H1/title recommendation, `seoTitle` only when needed, description, explicit `alternativeHeadline` if useful, tags, and any slug implication. Give one recommended package, not a keyword-combination lottery.
6. **On-page anchors:** orientation/standfirst and heading recommendations, each marked `safe`, `governing-skill approval required`, or `reject`. A proposed body fact must already be supported.
7. **Publication graph:** exact series metadata recommendation and exact predecessor/backward/forward/correction links, with a relationship reason and whether an older post needs a forward link.
8. **Link-worthy assets:** assets already in the article, who could cite/link to each and why, plus only earned missing-asset candidates. State `none` when appropriate.
9. **Distribution plan:** audience, channel, content-faithful hook, canonical/cross-post requirement, and desired contact behavior. Narrow pieces may choose one high-fit conversation over broad broadcasting.
10. **Stage ledger:** keep four outcomes separate: (a) doorway optimization changes recognition after an impression or landing, (b) distribution creates exposures, (c) link earning creates third-party references, and (d) actual reader contact is an attributable reply, correction, subscription, citation, referral, or conversation. Pageviews may be context, never proof of the other stages.
11. **Measurement/no-action rule:** evidence to observe, predeclared window, what decision it could change, and a valid `no change` outcome. Search crawler requests, ranking, impressions, clicks, links, and contact are different stages.
12. **Unknowns and rejection list:** unavailable query evidence, untested assumptions, bad-fit terms/channels, and edits that would flatten the piece.

# Proposed skill sections

1. Authority and non-goals
2. Required upstream shape contract
3. Freeze the article before looking outward
4. Classify broad, narrow, and hybrid portfolio roles
5. Extract content handles: named entities, problem, artifact, mechanism, evidence, stakes, time state, and reader gift
6. Translate handles into reader situations and honest intent; record wrong-fit intent
7. Design the doorway surfaces: H1, `seoTitle`, description, `alternativeHeadline`, tags, orientation/standfirst, and headings
8. Connect the publication graph: series and hard prose links
9. Audit link-worthy assets without manufacturing them
10. Match distribution to the article and desired contact
11. Separate eligibility/exposure/recognition/link/contact evidence and define a measurement window
12. Output schema, no-op outcome, and pre-publish/revision checklist
13. Conflict resolution and failure modes
14. Learning loop: article-specific lesson first; shared-rule update only after recurrence

# Method inside the skill

Use a content-first sequence: inventory what is actually present; freeze the center and claim limits; identify concrete handles; name the reader situation those handles truthfully serve; classify portfolio role; record excluded intent; then design metadata, on-page anchors, graph links, assets, and distribution. Query wording may sharpen a doorway only when it still denotes the same article. If the only apparent high-volume doorway would require a different article, reject it and either choose a narrower doorway or recommend a separate future article.

# Dependency order and conflict resolution

Standard publication order after the clean cutover:

1. `shape-article` — living center, form, protection.
2. Governing form skill: `personal-essays`, `blog-writing`, or `oss-radar`.
3. `shareable-engineering` when applicable — engineering evidence, trust, share value, and claim audit; genre-neutral title/GEO evidence is loaded as a reference, not independently applied.
4. `article-discovery-positioning` — outward translation once substance is stable.
5. `polish-prose` — sentence-level finish without semantic drift.
6. Final mechanical verification of frontmatter/series/link consistency; this is not another editorial pass.

If positioning reveals a missing factual anchor or necessary body context, return that single request to the governing skill, then regenerate the positioning brief. It may not silently edit around the upstream contract.

Conflict precedence: factual/source truth and explicit author context are hard constraints; `shape-article` wins on center/form; `personal-essays` wins on voice/aliveness/human spine; the governing skill wins on body structure and sourcing; AGENTS/build rules win on metadata, canonical, and series mechanics; the new skill owns doorway semantics inside those constraints; `shareable-engineering` supplies applicable evidence and engineering trust mechanics; `polish-prose` runs last but cannot change meaning.

# Anti-duplication cutover plan

1. Keep one normative owner per rule and replace duplicated prose with a one-line link/import.
2. `shape-article` keeps “serve humans and agents” and the protected-center contract, but delegates content-to-intent and doorway selection.
3. `blog-writing` keeps frontmatter shape, formatting, sourcing, canonical cross-post mechanics, and governing structures. It delegates semantic title/description/tag and distribution selection.
4. `personal-essays` keeps the exposed-register prohibitions and plain-doorway constraint. The new skill references them rather than restating the canon.
5. `shareable-engineering` keeps engineering trust/share mechanics and the evidence audit. Extract its genre-neutral Titles and Agentic Search evidence into one shared `references/discovery-evidence.md` (move, do not copy) or, until extraction, link to those named sections. The new skill owns application; both skills import the single evidence record. Remove doorway fields from the shareable pre-publish checklist or make them an explicit handoff to the new skill.
6. AGENTS keeps stable runtime/build semantics and compact routing: field audiences, `lastModified`, RSS, canonical, series schema, root-relative prose-link mechanics, and the learning loop. Move editorial diagnosis prose to the skill and link back instead of maintaining two evolving copies.
7. Update all skill descriptions and AGENTS order in the same cutover so “title,” “SEO/discoverability,” “distribution,” and “internal link positioning” route to only the new skill.
8. Never duplicate evidence statistics or citation claims in the core SKILL; imports carry their audit date and epistemic qualifiers.

# Missing rules the new skill must add

- A portfolio-role decision that values narrow identity/vision work and contextual hybrids, not only broad acquisition.
- An explicit no-search/no-`seoTitle`/no-change outcome when the honest doorway is already sufficient or search is not the piece's role.
- A wrong-fit intent list so impressions for docs/reference queries are not mistaken for qualified demand.
- A hard distinction among doorway optimization, distribution, link earning, and actual reader contact; none guarantees traffic.
- New-publication versus evidenced-revision modes, including the AGENTS query-data gate and a no-churn window.
- Keywords as honest semantic handles, not search-volume targets; tags remain 2–5 and power JSON-LD keywords.
- Form-sensitive heading rules and a governing-skill approval gate for every body change.
- A relationship test for series/prose links, bidirectional-link audit, and the ban on tag-only “related posts.”
- A link-worthy-asset test: integral, evidenced, reusable/citable, and valuable without the hoped-for link.
- Stage-specific evidence language: request/eligibility, selection/ranking, impression/exposure, click/landing, use/citation, referral, and human contact are not interchangeable.
- Role-sensitive success criteria so broad and narrow posts are not compared by raw pageviews.
- A rejection path when an attractive intent requires a different claim, different form, or separate article.

# Evidence

- `shape-article` explicitly runs before structure/polish, selects the governing form, protects unresolved material, and says discovery rules apply only where they fit.
- `personal-essays` makes voice and aliveness controlling, permits metaphor-forward H1s, bans manufactured feeling, and says exposed pieces need no `seoTitle` gymnastics.
- `blog-writing` owns frontmatter/format/sourcing/cross-post mechanics but currently also contains broad semantic title and description advice.
- `shareable-engineering` is scoped to engineering posts, yet currently contains genre-neutral title, description, search, and GEO evidence; it explicitly says personal voice wins.
- AGENTS already separates H1/OG title from `<title>`, defines metadata and update semantics, gates CTR changes on page-filtered query intent, distinguishes content/discoverability/distribution decisions, and defines series plus contextual prose links. It also says a standalone skill is justified only by repeated use and distinct automatic routing.

# Concrete skill implications

The implementation should create the standalone skill with the trigger and sections above; revise the global order; narrow `shareable-engineering` to engineering trust/share plus a shared evidence import; retain AGENTS as the authority for build/metadata/series mechanics; and require the structured positioning brief as the observable output. The skill is successful when it can return a truthful narrow or no-op doorway as readily as a broad one, and when every recommendation names which stage it may influence without claiming traffic or contact it cannot establish.
