The 0.3.0 pass removed the shared tadpole/comet glyph and made the four scenes structurally distinct, but that is not the same as compelling art direction. Do not call the current lab finished or impressive.

Known gaps from full-size screenshots:
- Ambient Drift is legible but too dim, uniform, and low-stakes; it lacks layered current, depth, and an authored large-scale composition.
- Memory Zone proves persistent semantic state, but still reads like an instrumentation diagram: a hard rectangle with many tokens. The before/inside/after journey needs stronger hierarchy and less debug-surface character.
- Text Emergence is the strongest editorial anchor, but the stem fragments are still a sparse technical proof. It lacks an authored peel/reform cycle, density shaping, and a moment worth holding.
- Fracture Pulse is materially better and debuggable, but remains a small centered crack study; it needs stronger topology hierarchy, controlled impact/afterglow, and an art-direction review before promotion.
- The dock is functional engineering instrumentation, not yet a beautiful museum instrument.

Required next pass:
1. Freeze deterministic/fixed visual checkpoints before every review.
2. Work scene by scene from composition and meaning, not primitive coverage.
3. Produce calm/active/decay full-scale screenshots plus motion clips where timing matters.
4. Get explicit owner approval for each scene; agent self-approval is not acceptance.
5. Only then promote a scene into an article preamble.

Acceptance: each scene is recognizable in grayscale by composition; carries its stated semantic meaning without explanatory copy; has a deliberate focal hierarchy and negative space; survives 1568px and mobile review; and receives explicit Goga sign-off. Technical truth, distinct geometry, and passing browser checks are necessary but insufficient.
