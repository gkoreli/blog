# Why we write

The publication exists to help Goga grow, help readers grow alongside him, and build in public by inviting a third-person eye into life and work. Writing is part of the work, not only a report written afterward.

# What belongs

Pain, ambiguity, and unresolved tension belong when alive. So do projects, architecture, methods, tenets, lessons, bets, plans, ambition, vision, and attempts to change the world. The publication does not use vulnerability as a quota or limit itself to one form.

# Time as editorial material

- Past: reflect, analyze, learn, name growth, and share earned lessons.
- Present: expose conditioning, ambiguity, calculated guesses, judgment calls, bets, pain, and what can still change.
- Future: declare intention, possibility, vision, and ambition that can still fail.

An article may use one layer or braid all three. Context chooses the governing form. A later continuation may write earlier lessons in the past tense and still be relatable about whatever new tension is alive then. Open-wound writing is permission to publish before hindsight closes the experience, not a rigid house style.
