The rule "no Sec-Fetch navigate/document means automation" is only safe if real readers arriving from t.co, google.com, and in-app browsers actually send those headers. Android WebView and iOS WKWebView are documented to omit them in some cases, and X in-app readers are a main channel. After at least one daytime cycle of traffic since 2026-09-03 01:35 UTC, break down browser-UA rows by referrer host, device type, fetch-metadata presence, Accept-Language presence, and ASN. Decide whether Accept-Language absence, fetch metadata absence, or hosting ASN each stands alone as an automation verdict, and record false-positive risk for each. This blocks TASK-0102.
