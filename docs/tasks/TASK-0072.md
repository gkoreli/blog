## Cross-case rules for the new skill

1. **Input starts after shaping.** Require `livingCenter`, `governingForm`, `portfolioRole` (`growth`, `identity`, or `contextual-hybrid`), `evidenceInventory`, `protectedMaterial`, and `unsupportedPromises`. The skill may translate these facts into doorways; it may not choose a new center or ending.
2. **Derive reader jobs from body evidence.** Inventory named entities, decisions, failures, procedures, comparisons, questions, and unique evidence. A reader job is valid only when a cold reader can complete it from the actual article.
3. **Generate handles by intersection, then exclusions.** A query handle must intersect (a) a reader job, (b) explicit body language/entities, and (c) the article's evidence boundary. Always emit negative handles—plausible queries the piece must not court.
4. **Assign each surface one job.** H1/OG protects the clicked reader's form; `seoTitle` makes the same substance legible to cold search; description names entities and a concrete promise; orientation/standfirst supplies context only when form permits; headings navigate actual decisions; tags label, not rank. Semantic overlap between H1 and `seoTitle` is required.
5. **Intervention depth varies by role/form.** Broad investigations can expose procedures and decision tables. Narrow project pieces can name projects and open decisions. Exposed essays normally permit metadata clarification and distribution framing only; no heading or body surgery. OSS Radar can lead with the named project and verdict only while evidence states and dates stay intact.
6. **Internal linking is editorial memory, not topical density.** Use `series` only for a deliberate sequence. Add one contextual prose link for a predecessor, sequel, correction, deeper dive, or product-boundary companion. Never link because tags overlap. Prefer linking *into* a protected essay from a later piece over altering its body.
7. **Separate four outcomes.** Doorway optimization improves fit/legibility for already-exposed results. Distribution deliberately places the article before communities. Link earning comes from unique evidence/assets others cite. Reader contact is a reply, correction, trace, referral, or continuing relationship. None guarantees the next.
8. **Name link-worthy assets already present.** Tables, reproductions, source-backed models, exact migrations, test protocols, vivid first-person passages, and unresolved decision records are candidates. Never manufacture a framework, quote, FAQ, or benchmark solely to attract links.
9. **No traffic guarantees.** Recommend a post-publication measurement window and page-filtered query/referral/contact evidence. Search impressions and crawler requests prove only their own stages. Without query data, recommendations remain content-fit hypotheses, not CTR diagnoses.
10. **Portfolio optimization is plural.** A narrow identity essay with ten right readers may be successful; a broad guide may carry acquisition; a hybrid may do both. Never rewrite every article toward the same audience role.

## Counterexamples the skill should reject

- Post 020 → “Best Privacy-Friendly Google Analytics Alternative”: no GA comparison, and the identifier is pseudonymous, not anonymous.
- Post 019 → “llms.txt Boosts AI Rankings”: directly contradicted by provider guidance and the stage model.
- Post 013 → “How to Stop Procrastinating and Choose One Project”: invents the solution the exposed essay refuses.
- Post 017 → “Herdr vs Superlogical: Which AI Multiplexer Is Best?”: one side had no public implementation to compare.
- Post 014 → “OpenWiki vs backlog-mcp: The Best Agent Memory Architecture”: changes a self-audit into a market verdict.
- Blanket rule → add H2s, FAQs, comparison slugs, keyword variants, and a 60-character `seoTitle` to every article. This corrupts form and can attract bad-fit intent.

## Concrete skill implications

The skill needs: a required role classifier; evidence-to-reader-job extraction; positive and negative query handles; per-surface doorway ownership; form-dependent intervention ceilings; meaningful series/cross-reference checks; explicit link-worthy-asset inventory; separate plans for doorway/distribution/link earning/contact; and a mandatory unknowns/confidence block that downgrades recommendations when no observed query data exists. It should include the three micro-examples above and the five rejection counterexamples so future agents learn not merely what to optimize, but when optimization becomes misrepresentation.
