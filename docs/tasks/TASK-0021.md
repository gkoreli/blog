Accepted ADR-0016.1: analytics exists to stop guessing about reach and distribution, not to become the blog's product. Every metric must support one of five actions: content correction, discoverability change, distribution change, bounded experiment, or no action. Reader contact, corrections, subscriptions, citations, and attributable links outrank page totals.

The ADR maps the existing architecture to five questions: external reach, content demand, acquisition channels, browser/automation composition, and whether a declared change produced a useful signal. It records exact current answers and their limits, defines a signal hierarchy, default observation windows, an eight-step decision loop, the RUM boundary, guardrails, non-goals, and the threshold for further analytics engineering.

Dated evidence included: 216 Browser events / 115 daily clients over 30d; 467 / 266 over 90d; 2,127 / 1,245 available history; leading recent paths; Reddit/Google/Hacker News/X/GitHub/ChatGPT referrers; and the largest traffic days.

Cross-references were verified for ADR-0016, ADR-0004, ADR-0005, analytics composition/query source, continuity migration, Worker root, stats methodology, and privacy disclosure. ADR-0016 now delegates purpose and stop conditions to ADR-0016.1. AGENTS.md records the same decision.
