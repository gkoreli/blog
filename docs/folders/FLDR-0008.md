One lane for ten initial posts, chosen 2026-09-03 (PROMPT 0013): agent-native publishing and first-party analytics for independent sites. Posts 016 (bring your own AI agent), 019 (llms.txt), 020 (first-party analytics), and the ADR-0016 thread provide the starting work. The lane turns the site's own measurements and engineering decisions into useful articles. Firsthand evidence identifies what was observed; it does not establish unique coverage, accurate readership, or guaranteed citations.

Rules for the lane (from `NORTH_STAR.md` "What Guides Readership" and `AGENTS.md` opening discipline):

- Shape each article around its actual question and evidence before choosing its form. Reference pages, investigations, field notes, and engineering arguments are valid. Make measurements dated and methods inspectable; do not force every article into a reference-page template.
- First paragraph gives the finding to a cold reader before any background. Title names the searchable subject.
- Each post ships with a research directory under `packages/blog/drafts/research/` and, where AI-assisted, a prompts file.
- Prepare relevant distribution copy and targets with the article. External posts, vendor messages, and upstream issues require scoped user authorization; this worklist does not authorize sending them.
- Reuse existing instrumentation and inspect its limits first. Add collection only for a demonstrated evidence gap. Necessary correctness and reliability repairs are not conditional on publishing three articles.

The following September 5 correction checklist was fulfilled; it does not reopen the article while the wider analytics system develops:

- Explain that Browsers counts requests satisfying a rule, with both false positives and false negatives possible. It is not a proven lower bound on people.
- Remove the universal under-two ratio threshold and the claim that all disagreement with a script counter is automation. Compare page views with page loads over an explicit common scope; daily clients and visits remain different units.
- Add the dated September 4–5 result: 372 UA-browser observations become 95 Browsers HTML views; comparable non-bot RUM records 14 page loads. This measures classification impact and remaining disagreement, not human-count accuracy.
- Correct signed-agent wording and the claim of IP-list validation. Existing signatures and named-UA classification remain useful evidence; neither proves that a person initiated each request. State the real verified-signature arrivals with deliberate probes separated.
- Verify the revised claims, preserve the original date and exact prompts, record a material update, and check the built page. Then close this revision.

**Correction versus continuation:** an error in an existing claim is corrected in the original article promptly. A new question with its own method and substantive result earns its planned follow-up. Add a short dated result and a contextual link to the predecessor when useful; do not transplant the entire new investigation into it. Stop expanding research when every claim actually made is supported, narrowed, rejected, or explicitly deferred. A known unresolved question is not an obligation to keep expanding article 024.
