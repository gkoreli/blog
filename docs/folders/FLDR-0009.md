Recurring review: inspect unreviewed referral aggregates during analytics reviews or a new spike. Record evidence before adding an exclusion; familiarity, website content, nationality, and a consumer ASN are not bot verdicts. A display approval never authenticates referral traffic. New-domain promotion is prevented even before review because public output uses a generic bucket.
