# What the founder actually did on X

Native-browser research checked October 17, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Narrative dates below use that timezone; CSV timestamps and monthly totals use UTC. Part of the [FluoTest investigation](README.md). Read alongside the [Reddit exchanges](reddit-interactions.md), [product-use evidence](signup-and-product-evidence.md), and [amplification history](amplification-analysis.md). The [89-entry post sample](x-post-sample.csv) preserves public timestamps, permalinks, and displayed counters; it contains no private analytics.

## The large viral account was someone else's

Dominik's profile displayed 71 followers, 112 following, 470 posts, and an April 2026 join date. His bio describes building FluoTest around baggage shifts at Schiphol. This is public self-description, not verification of his employment or schedule. The visible history is an effort to find users and builder peers, with many small responses. It does not resemble a founder who reliably commands a large X audience.

I read 89 distinct entries while scrolling his **Posts** tab back to April 16, including self-replies and the pinned entry. Their displayed views had a median of **51**; **69 of 89 were below 100**, and six were at least 500. The maximum in that sample was 2,052. These are observations of a rendered timeline, not an export of all 470 posts, a random sample, unique readers, or an engagement experiment. Views changed slightly during reading. The CSV keeps the first capture rather than silently mixing refreshed numbers.

The sample contains 42 April, 25 May, four June, ten July, two September, and six October entries by UTC date. It does not establish inactivity in August: the separately read Replies tab includes August conversations. X separates surfaces, and a profile's post count is not the number of original announcements.

This matters to Goga's concern: a product can report several hundred signups while its founder's ordinary X posts still get tens of views. The screenshot's 694,204-view event belongs to Hridoy's later publication. It cannot be assigned to Dominik's account, or translated into product visitors.

## Conversation 1: progress anxiety becomes a more useful accounting

The [65-user question](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2045824336117060017) is the largest post in the captured Posts sample. Its replies make the number more legible rather than simply celebrating it.

- [Zeeshan](https://x.com/zeeshana07x/status/2045841920400953747) shares different user and revenue totals from his own projects and offers encouragement. Dominik [answers](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2045842983736463447) that this is his first SaaS and he is comparing himself with experienced founders who already have networks. These are expressed perspectives, not evidence of anyone's underlying psychological state.
- [Andras](https://x.com/andraskindler/status/2045847836927934886) asks whether he is happy with the growth. Dominik [says](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2045855497606045870) that he is, but comparisons reduce the enjoyment. The social response offers a different criterion for evaluating progress.
- [Islas asks](https://x.com/JoelIslasx1/status/2045903323669090757) whether users pay and whether there is outreach. Dominik [clarifies](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2045922313074565337) that the tool is free and that 400 LinkedIn messages produced zero registrations. The paid-versus-free distinction was explicit in the original discussion, long before the viral headline called people customers.
- Asked about the niche, he [says](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2045927884234080336) he intended lead qualification, but most of these users were doing other things with quizzes. A signup count did not yet imply a settled customer definition.
- [Luca asks](https://x.com/byLuocca/status/2046175535923941781) about recurring versus one-off use and acquisition. Dominik [answers](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046215641816825898) that Reddit brought the initial users and blog content later brought AI referrals. This preserves the sequence missing from an accidental-growth summary.

Another [reply](https://x.com/yabsssai/status/2045874414336815409) discusses impressions and payout goals that do not fit his user question; he [responds with confusion](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2045874833536520207). That is a semantic mismatch. It is insufficient evidence to label the account automated or infer why it replied.

This conversation supplies several different forms of value: reassurance, comparative numbers, clarifying questions, and discovery of a product-positioning mismatch. Its 31 replies are not 31 people asking to buy quiz software.

## Conversation 2: an invitation brings a network, with uneven depth

The [April 20 invitation](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046250636295930317) brings introductions to a debugger, coding tools, design work, a content generator, a wellness platform, customer-intent software, and reciprocal-testing communities. These participants are responding to an opportunity to show their own work.

Some exchanges are specific. Dominik [asks Needle's builder](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046331741648949333) about actual users, revenue, and differentiation. He [says NicheIQ's homepage](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046322893030613412) is too technical for him, and [names a favorite design](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046333211249164518) in Stephanie's project. Those are concrete responses rather than interchangeable encouragement.

Other replies express intention rather than completed action. He [saves Nicholas's debugger for later](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046334019684470964), and [tells Vincent](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2046321482876244075) he will try the game later. No subsequent use is established by those words. The repeated Vincent handle appears again in July and August conversations, which supports continued public interaction; it does not prove friendship, product adoption, or a private relationship.

The commercial alignment is mixed. Builders of unrelated tools might become peers, sources, collaborators, or distributors. Many have no visible reason to need scored assessments. For Goga, this distinguishes a community conversation from an article's target-reader distribution. Both can be worthwhile, but an invitation to introduce projects naturally selects for people seeking exposure.

## Conversation 3: a product question prompts explanation and a reported change

In the [BetterLaunch announcement](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2058716642662633838), [Kat asks](https://x.com/kraniakat/status/2058725143543955598) how scoring works, what questions can be asked, and whether there are pictures or a demo. This is closer to a product comprehension problem than applause.

Dominik [explains](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2058828443207262558) points and weights, links the showcase, and says he has added more to the launch page. The visible sequence is question → explanation → reported presentation change. We did not acquire before/after captures of that launch page or observe Kat signing up.

Other replies remain social: [John](https://x.com/_JohnBuilds_/status/2058724728203067406) hopes it will bring users; Dominik [asks what he is building](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2058827437916426665). [Roxana](https://x.com/RoxanaLimban/status/2058835522303213614) congratulates him; he [points to the changelog](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2058910610369175734). A launch can produce both relationship maintenance and a useful product question, with different implications.

## Conversation 4: a directory placement has a visible social path

The history supplies a clearer chain for Nick Launches than merely finding FluoTest in a directory. [Nick invites him on May 25](https://x.com/nicklaunches/status/2058965171968807041), describing the visibility platform and suggesting his product would fit. Dominik [responds](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2058965372477301138) that he is on his way. On [June 1](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2061409660184203346), Dominik announces the placement and asks for support, then [tags three peers](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2061410270296100944) to recommend the platform. Nick's [June 7 recap](https://nicklaunches.com/blog/week-5-recap-june-07-2026/) includes FluoTest.

The evidence supports public invitation, expressed uptake, a listing, the founder's redistribution, and an editorial recap. It does not prove the invitation alone caused registration, paid promotion, or incremental quiz users. Nevertheless, it reveals distribution as a relationship with several public steps rather than a mysterious backlink appearing.

By July they are still talking: Dominik [questions Nick's Search Console graph](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2073064465650561392); Nick [explains the different axes](https://x.com/nicklaunches/status/2073064767699468352); Dominik [acknowledges his confusion](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2073065035660775766). This is a small example of a technical disagreement being resolved with a specific explanation. It does not validate Nick's wider acquisition claims.

## Conversation 5: useful participation can travel further than an announcement

In September, Dominik [reports a server error](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2094794673768255609) on Marc Lou's product in X's browser while Safari works. Marc [asks where](https://x.com/marclou/status/2094801275955155110); Dominik [narrows it to Google authentication](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2094801569237614932). His first bug report displayed **4,481 views**, greater than any entry in the 89-item Posts sample. It appears on the Replies surface, which is why it must not be used to alter that sample's maximum.

The exchange has informational content: a reproducible environmental difference and a narrowing question. A later [reply from Dominik](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2095009066590294029) says he chose to fix the equivalent problem in his own app. These are public statements, not a verified fix or downstream FluoTest conversions. The distribution mechanism remains uncertain: we observed a useful reply within a much larger account's conversation, not the platform's ranking decision.

Reach alone did not make every reply useful. His [June 25 product link](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2070087864415621513) beneath [a startup invitation](https://x.com/christophersaum/status/2069813347420119217) displayed 11 views while the parent had 342,813. Being under a popular post does not automatically transfer its audience. These are different posts under different conditions, not an experiment comparing link drops with bug reports.

## Conversation 6: asking what stayed with the reader

After [Shivam praises the growth story](https://x.com/ShivamhunXD/status/2061428189189251175), Dominik [asks what stayed in his head](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2061428518936793399). Shivam [answers](https://x.com/ShivamhunXD/status/2061430038088790243) that AI search and distribution interest him and that he intends to try the methods for his own project. Dominik [invites a later update](https://x.com/sikora_dominik_/status/2061433912610480137).

This is unusually relevant to Goga's publication. The founder moves from generic praise to a question about what the reader understood. The answer identifies the remembered subject: the acquisition tactic. It does not establish interest in quiz software or a successful replication. A short follow-up can reveal what a reader actually carried away, including when it differs from what the author hoped to convey.

## What this history supports

The founder tried progress reports, open invitations, directories, reciprocal support, practical feedback, product explanations, milestone stories, and AI demonstrations. Later posts include jokes and startup aphorisms with only a few or a few dozen views. There is no single visible format that reliably produces a large audience.

The human interactions have different objects: comparing progress, introducing oneself, clarifying a feature, diagnosing a bug, reciprocating support, recommending a launch surface, and borrowing an acquisition idea. A positive tone alone cannot distinguish them. A self-contained helpful answer may build a relationship even when a post does not create a buyer. A promise to try something should remain a promise until a later action is observed.

For Goga, the strongest proposed practice is to participate where the article's actual engineering problem is being discussed, contribute a specific finding that stands on its own, and use replies to discover confusion or missing evidence. Then distinguish a person applying the result from a person admiring the growth story. This investigation does not establish which practice would increase his readership; it gives more precise interactions to seek and measure.

## Coverage limits

Beyond the 89 Posts entries, I read selected full threads and 62 rendered nodes from the Replies surface, including ancestor posts by other authors. These sets overlap and must not be added to claim a unique total. The Replies reading reached May 25; it was not a complete historical export. Thread selection favored unusually discussed posts and interactions with substantive questions. Sentiment percentages, bot prevalence, follower conversion, actual link clicks, private conversations, and all deleted or hidden replies remain unavailable.

The public record supports expressed intentions and visible replies. It cannot fully recover private motives or what silent readers did. Hridoy's selective historical publishing pattern and commercial context are documented separately in the [amplification analysis](amplification-analysis.md); his entire X history was not exported.
