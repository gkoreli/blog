# North Star

Goga Koreli's personal publication covers engineering, personal experience, opinions, and philosophical inquiry. Writing should help the author understand his work and give readers useful explanation, evidence, or a precise account of experience.

Last revised: 2026-09-28.

## Publication Scope

Substantive practical articles connect transferable reader value with Goga's actual work and judgment. Retrospectives, guides, investigations, project visions, and unresolved personal essays are all valid. Choose the form from the material.

Keep navigation, identity, and typography consistent across the site. Article layouts may vary with their content. Infrastructure serves the publication's work; add it for a demonstrated need.

## Sections

- **Essays:** personal experience, opinions, and philosophical inquiry. Preserve the author's supplied voice and authorship boundaries.
- **Engineering:** technical explanations, investigations, decisions, build journals, and accounts of working with agents. Support both narrative articles and dense reference material.
- **OSS Radar:** the section remains functional and its existing URLs remain canonical. Its long-term placement and cadence are undecided; do not infer a decision from the number of published issues or begin a redesign to force one.

Essays and Engineering remain the core sections. Do not add a section until at least three existing posts need it and do not fit the current sections.

Photography belongs on a separate site if an actual body of curated photographs warrants one. Frames is excluded from this publication; a reserved domain is not a reason to build a photography site before the work exists.

## What Guides Design Work

- The homepage presents one featured article followed by a chronological stream. Section navigation belongs in the shared sidebar and section pages.
- Keep the sidebar available on article pages. Change the shared layout deliberately and preserve access to the rest of the publication.
- Review one requested surface at a time rather than expanding a change into a site-wide redesign.
- Use typography and spacing before decoration. Add an interactive element or custom layout when it helps the particular article.
- Preserve Essays and Engineering as the primary destinations. Keep OSS Radar reachable while its direction remains undecided.

## What Guides Readership

Undertake difficult, useful work when the question warrants it. Difficulty alone does not give readers a reason to care: explain the consequence, evidence, and relevance in language they can follow.

The owner's approximate portfolio aim is 80% readily understandable value and 20% specialist depth. This describes accessibility across the publication, not article-role quotas or a requirement to cut necessary technical detail. Within substantive practical articles, balance useful explanation with the author's own work and judgment.

Each title and opening should be understandable without prior familiarity with Goga. The body should provide reasons to assess its claims and show the author's projects, decisions, lessons, or open questions. Make the writing findable without changing it to promise content it does not contain.

## Open Decisions

Goga has not selected whether to retain OSS Radar as a separate section, reframe it, or place it within Engineering as a series. Preserve existing routes and avoid unnecessary design investment while that decision remains open.

Whether to use “builder log” in the publication's identity also remains undecided. Do not treat an open idea as implementation work.
