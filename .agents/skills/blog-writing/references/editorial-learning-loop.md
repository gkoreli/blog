# Editorial Feedback and Measurement

## Owner direction: growth through reader value

Goga's direction of October 18, 2026 is to prioritize sustained reader value and adoption when planning growth and distribution. Define the intended reader and useful continuation before choosing a success measure. For practical engineering work, seek specific evidence of understanding, application, corrections, recommendations to relevant peers, return reading, and confirmed subscriptions where appropriate. Treat impressions, likes, follower totals, and launch attention as distribution context; they do not establish these outcomes by themselves.

Prefer bounded learning with willing readers facing the actual question: understand their task, find where the explanation or artifact fails them, and improve the relevant work. Keep hypotheses separate from reported use and observed behavior. This direction does not impose utility metrics on personal essays, require positive feedback before publishing, or make difficulty itself a sign of valuable growth. Preserve the article's form and authorship.

## Owner direction: promotion deserves equal attention

Goga's direction of October 28, 2026 is to give adoption and distribution the same serious planning attention as building. For a growth request, do the concrete preparation: identify named destinations and readers, check current rules and comparable coverage, prepare usable copy or evidence, and define the next observable result. Do not substitute generic advice such as “share in relevant communities” for that work. Distinguish observed results, evidence of audience fit and untested ideas; do not call a tactic proven merely because a platform exists. This is a priority for planning, not a fixed time quota, a requirement to post everywhere, or authority to send unrequested messages.

Use the article's existing social directory for promotion work after its research cutoff. Keep exact drafts, proposed actions, publication receipts and measured outcomes separate. A completed article is not evidence of completed distribution; a completed strategy request also does not imply that its proposed posts have been authorized, published or scheduled. The [Bun AOT opportunity review](../../../../packages/blog/drafts/social/oss-radar-08-bun-aot/promotion-2026-10-28.md) is one dated example, not a permanent channel list.

## Publication feedback

After publishing a substantive engineering article:

1. Set a measurement window before interpreting performance.
2. Save page-filtered search queries, replies, corrections, attributable referrals, source changes, and relevant reports of use in the article's research directory.
3. Choose a content correction, metadata change, distribution change, experiment, or no action based on that evidence.
4. Apply the [metadata date rules](../../article-discovery-positioning/references/metadata-and-discovery.md#the-metadata-split) when the served page changes. Correct known factual errors without waiting for the performance window.
5. Keep article-specific lessons with the article. Update shared guidance for a recurring failure, an explicit owner direction, or a costly error caused by a missing rule.

When a willing reader is available, ask what they understood, where the explanation became difficult, and what evidence they still need. Record the feedback and editorial decision. Reader feedback is useful but is not a mandatory release gate; agent review does not establish human reception.

Store private messages outside Git, preserving original bytes and the receipt date. Use a descriptive filename and a worklist reference. Do not infer a conversation date from clock times alone. Public notes may summarize advice and resulting changes without republishing the conversation.
