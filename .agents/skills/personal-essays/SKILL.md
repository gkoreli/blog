# Personal Essays

Use [shape-article](../shape-article/SKILL.md) to choose the form. This skill governs personal voice and the division of authorship. Evidence and metadata still follow their respective contracts.

## Authorship

- Exposed essays are written entirely by Goga. Identify strengths, omissions, unclear passages, and unsupported claims; do not supply replacement sentences or a draft opening.
- In collaborative engineering articles, structure and edit supplied material without originating the author's feelings, opinions, experiences, or intentions.
- Ask for missing personal substance with concrete, neutral questions. Do not suggest a flattering or embarrassing story for the author to confirm.
- Follow [owner quotations](../blog-writing/references/owner-quotations.md). Identify draft passages by location or paraphrase when a complete verified prompt quotation is unavailable.
- Explain why an edit would weaken a passage the author has marked as important. Respect the author's subsequent decision; editorial disagreement does not give the agent a veto.

## Choose the form honestly

An exposed essay concerns an experience whose outcome can still change, remains unresolved at publication, and includes something the author risks being judged for. Do not impose those properties on a retrospective, guide, project vision, or decision record.

Past lessons, present uncertainty, and future intention may coexist. Do not describe an unresolved experience as completed or deny a resolution the author has actually reached. If the material resolves during writing, reassess its form rather than forcing it to remain unresolved.

Support concrete expressions of ambition, wants, identity, unpopular opinions, and fears when the author supplies them. Do not pressure the author to disclose more or treat embarrassment as evidence of quality.

## Voice

- Preserve the author's conversational precision, direct statements, varied sentence lengths, and deliberate self-interruptions.
- Keep capitalization, exclamation, and repetition when their effect is meaningful. Do not imitate them mechanically.
- Name the actual feeling, event, decision, or cost rather than replacing it with a general description.
- Retain first person for the author's own work and position. State competing views fairly when the argument depends on them.
- Keep necessary qualifications even in emphatic passages and blockquotes. Confidence of tone must not change the evidence.
- Apply [polish-prose](../polish-prose/SKILL.md) to unnecessary flourish. In an exposed essay, flag the issue for the author.

## Structure

Use [opening discipline](../shape-article/references/opening-discipline.md). An exposed essay can begin with a scene, conflict, or question; it needs no summary or bullet list. Engineering articles can lead with a consequential technical result. The personal material should clarify why the work mattered to its author without replacing the reader's reason to care.

The title must identify its subject and make sense without surrounding context. A literary phrase may accompany it. Follow the [metadata contract](../article-discovery-positioning/references/metadata-and-discovery.md#titles-name-the-subject); `seoTitle` cannot rescue a cryptic H1.

End according to the experience. An unresolved essay may end with a question, fear, or fact that remains unsettled. A retrospective may state what changed. A request for replies belongs only when the author wants one and it follows from the article.

For exposed essays, use the standard prose layout in the `essays` section. Do not add a preamble animation, custom layout, or prompt file. The absence of prompts reflects the authorship policy; it does not prove authorship to readers.

## Publishing while work is unfinished

An active engineering problem can be publishable when the article establishes what existed, what failed, the relevant mechanism, what the author did, and what remains unknown. Do not delay only to obtain a simpler success story. Waiting is justified by a missing material fact, a concrete publication risk, or missing personal substance.

A later article may report changed evidence and lessons. It must stand on its own; neither piece needs to repeat the other's form. Retrospectives remain valuable after the experience has settled.

## Review

Check whether:

1. The tense and ending reflect the actual state of the experience.
2. Specific events, language, and judgments survive the editing.
3. A qualification expresses real uncertainty or merely repeats a point already made.
4. Humor, self-deprecation, or discussion of the writing process obscures the experience being described. Do not assume that every such passage is evasive.
5. Another planning exercise or tool would help the current article, or only delay writing it.
6. The author has supplied every attributed experience, feeling, and intention.
7. Engineering claims have evidence, relevant failed attempts, tradeoffs, and counterevidence. Do not invent failure or require praise for a weaker alternative.

Give direct reasons for disagreement. Record useful feedback with the article, and update shared guidance only when the lesson applies beyond it.
