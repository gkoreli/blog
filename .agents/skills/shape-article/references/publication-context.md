# Publication Purpose

Goga Koreli writes about building with agents, engineering decisions, and personal experience. Articles should help readers understand something useful and show the work and judgment behind it. [NORTH_STAR.md](../../../../NORTH_STAR.md) defines the publication's scope.

## Substantive practical articles

Use [earned-trust-writing](../../earned-trust-writing/SKILL.md): give roughly equal substantive weight to transferable reader value and Goga's actual work, experience, opinions, mistakes, and open questions. Connect the explanation to the experience throughout the article. This is not a word-count quota.

Teach directly from evidence and experience. Keep external findings, firsthand observations, interpretations, and proposed experiments distinct. Ask concise questions for missing personal material; never invent it. Before delivery, check that the article is useful without the byline, specific to Goga's work, and stronger because the two perspectives are connected.

The [bot-detection article](../../../../packages/blog/posts/024-how-i-separate-readers-from-bots-without-javascript.md) demonstrates explanation supported by implementation, measurements, corrections, and limits. Use that relationship where appropriate; do not copy its structure or claim measurements that were not made.

## Forms and time

Retrospectives, guides, investigations, build journals, decisions, project visions, and personal essays are valid forms. Choose from the material. Past experience can support a lesson; present uncertainty can remain unresolved; future intent must remain a proposal. These can coexist in one article.

Exposed essays remain author-written under [personal-essays](../../personal-essays/SKILL.md). They do not require a tutorial, lesson, or resolved ending.

## Publication roles

| Role | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Reader-growth | Explains a problem, method, decision, or artifact useful beyond the existing audience |
| Signature | Makes Goga's thinking, voice, projects, or intentions specific and understandable |
| Bridge | Connects a recognizable reader problem to Goga's work or judgment |

Roles describe articles; they do not rank their quality or impose a publication quota. Use [article-discovery-positioning](../../article-discovery-positioning/SKILL.md) after choosing the form.

Core subjects include `@nisli/core`, `backlog-mcp`, agent workflows, TypeScript, monorepos, and open-source maintenance. Publish on `gkoreli.com` first. When distribution is requested, choose the relevant channel; cross-posts must point their canonical URL to the original.
