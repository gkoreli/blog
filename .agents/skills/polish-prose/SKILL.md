# Polish Prose

Edit sentences for clarity and precision after the article's form is settled. Preserve facts, qualifications, technical terms, code, and the author's meaning.

## Rules

- Prefer a literal phrase when it conveys the meaning. Keep a metaphor only when the literal alternative would lose something necessary; avoid familiar figures of speech and chains of metaphors.
- Use familiar, precise words. Remove repetition, filler, and introductory sentences that add no information.
- Prefer active voice when the actor is known. Vary sentence length and punctuation without adding a pattern for its own sake.
- Retain uncertainty and limits that affect the claim. Remove empty hedges without turning an estimate, opinion, or proposal into a fact.
- Represent an opposing position accurately. Avoid repeated “not X, but Y” constructions; use at most one per piece unless the reasoning requires more.
- Use two examples as a starting point when examples help. Include more when the evidence or explanation requires them; do not add examples solely for rhythm.
- Avoid neighboring paragraph endings written as punchlines. State the point without announcing the next section.
- Preserve deliberate fragments, self-interruptions, repetition, capitalization, and roughness when they convey the author's thought. Flag unclear passages rather than assuming smoothness is always better.

## Review

Identify protected text, edit the prose, then compare the result for changes in meaning, emphasis, certainty, or voice. Restore any edit that reduces precision or erases meaningful uncertainty. Read aloud to check rhythm and clarity.

Keep quotations exact. For Goga's attributed words, follow [owner quotations](../blog-writing/references/owner-quotations.md). A rewritten passage must not remain presented as a verbatim quotation.

For an exposed essay, identify problems for the author; do not draft or replace prose. For a review-only request, report findings rather than rewriting. The governing skill controls structure and authorship; these rules control sentence-level clarity.
