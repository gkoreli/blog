# Honest X launch learning loop

The objective is not to reverse-engineer an unknowable ranking system from one post. It is to learn which truthful doorway repeatedly creates reader contact for this publication.

## Before publishing

Build a small comparable baseline from 10–20 recent original posts. Exclude replies and pure reposts. Mark quote posts separately. Record snapshots at comparable ages whenever timestamps allow.

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Post | Canonical status URL and publish time |
| Age at snapshot | Exact elapsed time |
| Doorway | Result, tension, question, identity, artifact, or announcement |
| Media | None, image, GIF, native video, or article card |
| Link placement | Main post, reply, both, or none |
| Visible response | Views, likes, replies, reposts, bookmarks when visible |
| Qualified contact | Specific technical replies, corrections, citations, article discussion, or messages |
| Audience spread | Known responders only, adjacent community, or clearly outside the usual audience |

Compute medians, not averages, for comparable original posts. One outlier should not redefine the baseline.

## Freeze the experiment

Choose one primary change per launch, such as native video versus GIF, evidence-first versus personal-first opening, or link in the main post versus a final reply. Multiple simultaneous changes may still be necessary for a good launch, but then record the result as observational rather than causal.

Do not edit copy repeatedly from early noise. Repair factual errors, broken links, inaccessible media, or an obviously broken crop immediately. Otherwise freeze until the planned observation window ends.

## Snapshots

When practical, record:

- about one hour: delivery and immediate reader comprehension;
- about 24 hours: the main distribution comparison;
- about seven days: durable saves, replies, search/discovery, and article contact.

Do not treat views alone as success. Separate:

- **delivery**: X showed the post;
- **engagement**: someone reacted on X;
- **reader contact**: someone offered useful experience, correction, or discussion;
- **article contact**: attributable visits or replies that reference the article;
- **durability**: saves, later reshares, citations, or continued discovery.

## Local operating labels

These are working heuristics for this account, not universal definitions and not promises:

- **Above baseline:** exceeds the rolling median of comparable original posts at the same age.
- **Breakout:** reaches roughly 5× that median and creates qualified contact beyond the usual responders.
- **Viral for the account:** reaches roughly 10× that median, earns voluntary reshares outside the known audience, and produces article or reader contact rather than views alone.

Never present these labels as proof that a media format or hook caused the result.

## Decision record

End `metrics.md` with:

```markdown
## Decision

- Keep:
- Change next time:
- Evidence:
- Confidence and confounders:
- Article correction needed: yes/no
- Shared-skill change needed: yes/no
```

Update the shared skill only after the same lesson appears across launches or when one missing safeguard caused a costly failure. A topic-specific outcome belongs in the launch record.
