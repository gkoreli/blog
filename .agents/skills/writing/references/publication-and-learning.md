# Publication and learning

Use when preparing an article for release, planning requested distribution or interpreting its reception. A small prose edit does not require all of these stages.

## Release the claims actually made

Verify consequential numbers, dates, quotations, attributions, technical statements and links at their sources. Keep observations, interpretations and proposals distinguishable. Preserve counterevidence and conditions that change the result. Read the title, description, opening, headings, main visual and ending together; they must deliver the same subject and earned scope.

Check metadata and prompt policy, formatting, canonical/internal relationships and actual generated HTML. For representations or custom layouts, inspect the Markdown and other claimed outputs too. Check the social card when part of the release. Identify expected pre-push 404s when the release creates their targets. Use blog-maintenance for scoped validation, isolation, Git identity and delivery; never run a production build into another session’s active output.

A local reproduction, source delivery, deployment activation, served behavior and reader outcome are distinct evidence. Do not use one successful endpoint to certify a broader flow. Human feedback can improve the article but is not a universal release gate; agent review does not establish human reception.

An unfinished outcome can be published when its current claims are supported. Do not wait only for a completed adoption curve or force a resolved answer onto an inquiry. Research-footprint freezing is optional provenance work, not a release-quality badge.

## People and agents are both audience

[ADR-0016.19](../../../../docs/adr/0016.19-agent-mediated-audience-growth.md) governs positive recognition of agent access, discovery, citations, distribution and use. Missing human identity limits attribution, not the existence of the observed result. A known-URL retrieval, an unseeded search selection, a supported answer and an external copy are different outcomes; choose evidence appropriate to the one claimed.

Comparable growth requires comparable windows, methods, exclusions and source composition. Keep controlled trials outside organic growth. Direct replies, subscriptions and human/device calibration apply to their own questions; none is a universal condition for recognizing agent-mediated reach or usefulness. Our own deployment is not independent operator adoption.

## Requested distribution deserves concrete work

When growth or distribution is requested, give it the same serious planning attention as building: identify named readers and destinations, check current rules and comparable coverage, prepare exact usable copy/evidence, and define the next observable result. This priority is not a fixed time quota, a requirement to post everywhere or permission to send messages.

Publish on `gkoreli.com` first; authorized cross-posts use its canonical URL. Choose the channel and copy for the article. A copy, preview or strategy request does not authorize posting. Keep draft, reviewed, published and measured states separate. Use `launch-article-on-x` for its media/account/publication mechanics and `run-discovery-experiment` for a bounded discovery trial rather than duplicating their procedures here.

For X, follow the owner’s [conversation and useful-outreach direction](../../launch-article-on-x/references/publication-verification.md#x-conversations-and-useful-outreach): respond to people, understand their engineering problem and connect the relevant implementation or finding. Draw from the [public project index](../../../../docs/reference/owned-open-source-projects.md) as well as articles; the useful source may be code, package docs, an example or a question that changes the decision.

For Hacker News, check current guidelines before a requested submission; titles must remain faithful to the article. **HN comment composition stays with the author.** One submission’s reception does not isolate a title effect.

### Owner direction: GitHub outreach needs an invitation

Do not use another project’s GitHub issues for article promotion or trial recruitment unless the thread is an RFC or explicitly invites outside engagement. Verify that invitation before proposing or drafting outreach. A relevant topic, open issue or maintainer’s internal experiment is insufficient. This restriction concerns promotion/recruitment, not ordinary issue work.

Store post-release promotion in the article’s existing social directory, separate from its frozen research footprint. A completed article does not establish completed distribution; a prepared strategy does not establish authorized or executed posts.

## Learn from the actual result

Declare a measurement window before interpreting performance. Save relevant page-filtered search reports, replies, corrections, attributable referrals, source changes, citations and reports of use. Impressions, likes and page totals give distribution context without establishing comprehension, endorsement or application.

Choose a content correction, metadata change, distribution repair, bounded experiment or no action from the evidence. Correct known factual errors immediately; do not wait for the performance window. Update modification metadata only when served content changes materially. A review date is not an automation or evidence that a capture happened.

When a willing reader is available, ask what they understood, where the explanation failed and what evidence they need. Preserve feedback and the editorial decision. Keep private messages outside Git with original bytes and receipt date; do not infer dates from clock times alone. Public notes may summarize the advice and resulting change without republishing the conversation.

Keep one-off lessons with the article. Update shared guidance for an explicit owner direction, a recurring failure or a costly error caused by a missing rule. Do not convert one launch into universal platform advice.
