# Personal authorship

Use for personal essays and personal material inside practical articles.

## Keep the author’s division of work

Exposed essays are written entirely by Goga. Identify strengths, omissions, unclear passages and unsupported claims; do not draft a new opening or supply replacement sentences. For collaborative engineering articles, structure and edit supplied personal material without originating feelings, opinions, experiences or intentions.

Ask neutral, concrete questions for missing personal substance. Do not suggest a flattering or embarrassing story for the author to confirm. Explain when a proposed edit would weaken a passage the author has marked as important, then respect the author’s decision. Editorial disagreement is not an agent veto.

An attributed quotation must reproduce the complete verified message; use location or paraphrase for draft feedback when that is unavailable. Confidence, a blockquote or forceful punctuation cannot strengthen the evidence.

## Let the actual experience choose the form

An exposed essay concerns supplied experience whose outcome remains live and in which the author risks judgment. Do not impose those properties on retrospectives, guides or project visions. If the material resolves while writing, reconsider the form rather than forcing uncertainty to remain.

Support supplied ambition, wants, identity, fears and unpopular opinions without pressuring more disclosure or treating embarrassment as quality. Past lessons, present uncertainty and future intention may coexist. A resolved experience can support a direct conclusion; an inquiry can retain its question.

## Preserve the voice and stakes

Keep conversational precision, direct statements, meaningful repetition, varied sentences and deliberate interruptions. Name the actual event, feeling, choice or cost rather than replacing it with generalities. Do not imitate capitalization or exclamation mechanically. Keep first person for the author’s own work and position; represent competing views fairly when needed.

The title must identify the subject and make sense on its own. An exposed essay can open with a concrete author-supplied scene, conflict or question whose stakes are clear immediately. Do not impose an agent-written summary, findings list or technical lesson. Give feedback about an unclear opening instead of rewriting it.

End according to the experience. A question, fear or unresolved fact may be appropriate; a retrospective may say what changed. Add a request for replies only if the author wants one and the piece supports it. Humor or writing-process discussion is not automatically evasive; judge its actual effect.

Exposed essays use the standard prose layout in the `essays` section: no custom layout, animated preamble or prompt file. Prompt omission reflects the authorship policy and is not proof of human authorship.

## Review without turning preparation into delay

Check tenses, specific events, supplied judgments, meaningful qualifications and the actual ending. Engineering claims inside personal work still need evidence and relevant tradeoffs. Do not invent a failure or require praise for a weaker alternative.

An active practical problem can be publishable with its known state and remaining uncertainty. Wait for missing consequential evidence, concrete publication risk or essential personal substance, rather than for a simpler success story. Another tool or planning exercise should help the current piece, not merely postpone it.
