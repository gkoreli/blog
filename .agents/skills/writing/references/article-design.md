# Article design

Use for a new article or substantive structural revision. Ordinary wording fixes do not need this process.

## Find the article that the material supports

Establish the main reader problem, claim, experience or live question; the reason this author can address it; the evidence; and the passages or uncertainty that must survive. Ask only for consequential missing material, using concrete neutral questions. Do not invent a motive or feed the author a flattering or embarrassing story to confirm.

| Form | Develop | End according to |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering explanation or decision | Useful mechanism, actual choices, evidence and tradeoffs | Supported lesson or decision |
| Evidence-led investigation | Owned observation and falsifiable question; test competing explanations | What changed, what remains open, what would change the conclusion |
| Field note or build journal | Dated attempt, finding, failure or blocker | Actual current state and useful next question |
| Personal inquiry or exposed essay | Supplied experience, contradictions and stakes | The author’s experience, including unresolved questions |
| OSS Radar | Shipped product, tested claims and product thesis | Bounded adoption advice or cohort thesis |

Choose the governing form before imposing an outline. Forms can mix; do not force every piece into a success story, tutorial, confession or unresolved ending.

## Connect reader value and the author’s work

For substantive practical work, weave transferable explanation through concrete author-present material: what Goga built, tried, chose, observed, corrected or still cannot resolve. Roughly equal substantive weight is a judgment about the article’s substance, not a numerical target.

Use these checks:

- **Without the byline:** can a stranger understand or use the result and inspect its reasons?
- **Without generic explanation:** do the remaining choices, artifacts and questions identify this author doing this work?
- **Together:** does the explanation help interpret the experience, and does the experience test or qualify the advice?

State opinions as the author’s judgments when supplied. A sensible proposed method may be useful while its outcome remains unknown. Do not invent failure, emotion or praise for an alternative to make a lesson appear earned.

## Organize for understanding

For explanatory engineering and OSS work, open with the supported what and a short set of findings the body delivers. Bring in background only when it helps explain them. Each section should make its point plain and leave a takeaway or live question; use this as a reading check rather than a paragraph template.

Introduce implementer detail when the reader needs it. Use prose for connected reasoning, lists for parallel or ordered information, and tables or diagrams for relationships that become clearer visually. Do not add interactivity or a visual solely for variety. Keep content readable without JavaScript.

Preserve concrete admissions, meaningful repetition, conversational precision and deliberate interruptions. Improve accidental disorder without erasing the author’s supplied uncertainty or turning distinctive prose into a generic explainer.

## An unfinished result can still be publishable

An article can report what existed, what happened, what was done and what remains unknown while work continues. Do not wait only for cleaner hindsight or a completed adoption curve. Wait for a missing material fact, a concrete publication risk or essential personal substance. A later article can report changed evidence without rewriting the earlier state.

For continuation links or a series, identify the specific relationship: predecessor, correction, deeper mechanism, adjacent boundary or next step. Shared tags do not establish a reading sequence. Reader-growth, signature and bridge are useful editorial roles, not quality ranks or publication quotas.
