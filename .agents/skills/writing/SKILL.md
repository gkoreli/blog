## Preserve authorship and scope

- Do not invent Goga’s experiences, feelings, intentions, judgments, quotations or completed outcomes. Supplied uncertainty and deliberate voice can be part of the work.
- Exposed essays are author-written: give editorial feedback without drafting replacement prose. They have no custom animated preamble. Raw shaping prompts and personal working notes stay private for all article forms, including essays, collaborative engineering articles and OSS Radar. Do not create public prompt records or pages.
- Practical articles should teach something independently useful through Goga’s inspectable work and judgment. Give reader value and author-present substance roughly equal weight as an editorial judgment, never a word-count quota. Do not impose that form on an essay, poem or narrow reference.
- Choose the form that fits the actual experience. Past lessons, unresolved questions and future intentions may coexist. An inquiry need not end in a verdict; a resolved result need not be made uncertain.

## Make the useful what immediate

For every article, use an attention-grabbing, concrete title and lead with the strongest supported result, problem or payoff in ordinary language. Avoid a preface or research chronology before the point. Introduce specialist terms when needed and explain them plainly.

The useful what must be clear within the first **50 words** across title, `seoTitle`, description and introduction. Check each real entry surface too: shared title/description, search title/description, rendered title/opening. A surprising hook must retain its population and consequential limits. Exposed essays can lead with an author-supplied scene or conflict; this rule does not authorize an agent to draft them.

## Keep claims inspectable

Separate what was observed, reproduced, inspected in code, documented, reported, inferred or proposed. Put the evidence and limits beside consequential claims. A successful local test, deployment activation and current production behavior are separate results.

People and agents are both audience. Count supported access, discovery, citation, distribution and use at their observed stages under [ADR-0016.19](../../../docs/adr/0016.19-agent-mediated-audience-growth.md). Missing human identity does not erase agent reach; a request does not establish comprehension or a human trigger.

## Finish in proportion to the task

For substantive articles, verify the claims, sharpen the title and opening, then polish. Check that the title, headings, body and ending deliver the same subject and earned scope. Use the selected mode’s references for its actual checks; do not require every reference for every task.

For repository source, rendering, isolated builds, checks and delivery, use [blog-maintenance](../blog-maintenance/SKILL.md). Publication and external messages stay within the user’s authorization; a request for copy or strategy does not authorize posting.
