## Cohort interpretation to test in prose

The sample contains several attempts to make existing workflows cheaper to operate or easier to inspect, through narrower contracts: shared decision context, a shared route description, a saved conversation identity, a durable workflow program. These are mechanisms actually present in the sample, not a claim about all November launches or growth in their markets.

Three useful judgments: specialist execution can beat a general interface when the request shape is constrained; agent infrastructure must preserve intent/context as well as produce output; ownership depends on concrete dependency and substitution paths. Rival interpretation: HN plus the direct-GitHub threshold preferentially exposes developer infrastructure. Durable advantage weakens when model/host vendors absorb the specialized capability, when compatibility obligations outrun maintainers, or when operational burden exceeds saved integration work. No unsupported survival forecast or numerical ranking is needed.
