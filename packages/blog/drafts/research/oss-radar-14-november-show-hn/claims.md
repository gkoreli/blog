## Direction and editorial limits

- The manuscript describes shipped code/instructions at the pinned revisions. It makes no claim that a prototype, symbol or extension point is an available product capability without following its implementation.
- The continuing-control thesis is an editorial hypothesis. It is not a maintainer roadmap or a market forecast.
- Proposed crash, recovery, browser and hook trials have not run. They remain questions in the article, not completed work or commitments for the next session.
- Source inspection cannot prove the absence of bypasses, arbitrary external effects, telemetry, vulnerabilities or alternative deployment paths. Reader copy does not make those negative claims.
- No comparative vendor pricing, legal advice, security guarantee, performance ranking, maintenance prediction, audience growth or user adoption is inferred.
