## Retained prior art and exclusions

The earlier review includes Mailchimp, Kit, Buttondown, listmonk, ALTCHA, Spamhaus, M3AAWG, and a 2026 author-hosted study. They informed the decision space; the current article does not need another vendor comparison. No provider migration or replacement verifier was selected.

The study's publication metadata and peer-review status remain unverified. The M3AAWG-linked header proposal is an expired Internet-Draft, not an RFC. The reported ALTCHA failure is a bounded operator account; its library advisory must not be attributed to unaffected implementations. Provider marketing, repository existence, and a small subscriber count establish no efficacy benchmark.
