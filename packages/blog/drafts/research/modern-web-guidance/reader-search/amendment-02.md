# Post-validation interface repairs and limits

November 14, 2026. The reserved matrix and hierarchy runs completed and remain unchanged. With focused hybrid passage candidates, validation article-at-three was 31/32 short topics, 31/32 framed questions and 8/8 additional recollections. Focus gained and lost cases; it did not provide a net article-at-three improvement over raw hybrid in the paired forms. Three selected paragraphs showed the frozen target block for 23/32 topics and 23/32 framed questions. This is not a comprehensive paragraph-utility score.

Review exposed a consequential label-construction limit in the separate recollections: their block IDs were inherited from each article's other topic, sometimes a different aspect. In particular, the Buzz shared-model recollection inherited a signed-key paragraph. Its article label is usable; that inherited block is not a valid exact-paragraph answer target. Preserve every row, but do not interpret a 3/8 inherited-block count as recollection answer quality. Post-hoc source review will identify useful alternatives and actual misses separately, without replacing labels or publishing a corrected accuracy percentage.

Two interface changes follow the observed browser trials, **after validation**:

1. Query focus v1 discarded project/tool/library qualifiers inside a recognized topic. A counterfactual browser query showed that keeping the project category changed the article ranking. `query-v2.mjs` removes only location framing and preserves those qualifiers. All 114 measured query texts are byte-equivalent under v1/v2; the pack did not exercise that defect. New targeted regression checks establish the text contract, not unseen relevance gains.
2. The UI adds optional context from the three passage matches that produced an article candidate. This lets a reader inspect another relevant section when the three selected blocks miss the needed aspect. It is a larger text budget and cannot be folded into the frozen three-paragraph result. Article ranking and the selected-block algorithm remain unchanged.

The final interface also defaults to the whole question. Topic focus did not improve validation hybrid article-at-three in aggregate and reduced the framed-query selected-block count from 26/32 to 23/32 (development: 12/14 to 10/14). Focus remains an explicit option. This is a post-validation product decision between already measured conditions, not an untouched validation estimate of the final choice. No per-query rule, score weight, label or ranking was changed.

Exact-paragraph navigation was independently observed in the actual browser: an animated-icon query found OSS Radar #01, and clicking the source link scrolled the live page to its Moving Icons paragraph and visibly highlighted the target text. The initially supplied emoji topic still has no verified article answer: neither the source snapshot nor eligible live article text contained a literal emoji-project term. Related icons are a possible interpretation, not a confirmed answer or an authorized alteration of article content.
