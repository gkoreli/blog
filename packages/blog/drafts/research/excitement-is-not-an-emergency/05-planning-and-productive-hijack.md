## Governing form and center

Earned-Trust practical inquiry, section `essays`. The agent may shape the supplied substance and source the factual claims under the existing collaboration rules; this is not an entirely author-written exposed essay.

Center: meaningful work can become permission to displace other priorities. The author wants to make room for creative energy deliberately without repeatedly overriding sleep, primary work, health, or time with other people.

Distinctions to preserve:

1. Importance over a life or a multi-year ambition does not automatically settle immediate priority.
2. Capture records the current state; experimentation creates further understanding. A ten-minute narrative cannot pre-record discoveries that require coding, testing, and reading.
3. A plan can specify an occasion, starting action, and boundary; it cannot guarantee a chosen emotional or chemical state.
4. Trial success requires both meaningful creative engagement and protected commitments. Fewer ideas, more notes, or sleep obtained by indefinitely abandoning personal work would not establish the desired result.

Planning now precedes the interruption rule in the article. **SURGE → CAPTURE → RETURN → REVISIT → CHOOSE** remains unchanged and explicitly proposed. The ending changes from asking what the author is willing to leave unexplored to the live test of giving both ambitions and the rest of life deliberate time. It must not turn the owner's desire to keep both into a falsely solved tradeoff.
