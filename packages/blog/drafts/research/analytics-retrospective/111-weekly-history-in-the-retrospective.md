## Primary references and boundaries

- [Cloudflare worker-first routing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/routing/worker-script/) supports placing the collector before static delivery, not guaranteed persistence.
- [RFC 9110 §10.1.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-10.1.3) permits missing Referer; absence does not identify acquisition.
- [Exa’s crawler documentation](https://crawler.exa.ai/) distinguishes search crawling from ExaUser’s requested retrieval. The old detail link redirects to a different origin that the research tool could not retrieve. The accessible homepage supports the narrowed role claim; retrieval failure is not an outage or a new signing-origin policy.
- [Ahrefs’ robot documentation](https://ahrefs.com/robot) supplies declared crawling/auditing roles. Neither this catalogue nor a signature establishes an observed human trigger.
- [RFC 9421](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9421.html) makes covered components explicit; the article cross-references the three-request and 52-proof retained experiments. No native-verifier agreement or full conformance claim.
- [D1 metering definitions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/#definitions) distinguish scanned work from audience size; the historical measured comparison remains dated.
- [sendBeacon’s return contract](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/sendBeacon#return_value) is queue admission, not receiver delivery. This supports the separate blocked-beacon controls, not an explanation of the historical gap’s proportions.
- [Search Console’s performance documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553) identifies query/page evidence still needed. Public docs are not account measurements; the pending Google-origin approval is not bypassed.

These primary sources were checked October 30. Own research links document methods and receipts; they are not independent corroboration. Privacy/legal completeness, source operators, human/device calibration, native comparison and authenticated search/usefulness remain open. Human trials and the normal owner-browser check stay deferred.
