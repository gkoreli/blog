A versioned registry supplies stable IDs, exact canonical paths, optional declared aliases and singular article/evidence parent relationships. Each selected event has one unique opaque reference, stored kind and measurement provenance, timestamp and supplied inclusion assessment. Count it once under article, evidence, site or unmapped. Evidence access stays separate from article events; related-resource totals are not unique audience or comprehension. Excluded and unassessed events remain in the selection denominator.

Reject conflicting IDs/path ownership, duplicate events, unsupported schema/limits and recognized timestamps outside the declared UTC window. Titles are labels, not identities. ## Prior art and design inference

[Plausible pageview properties](https://plausible.io/docs/custom-props/for-pageviews) keep an event's type and attached dimensions separate. [Matomo content tracking](https://developer.matomo.org/guides/content-tracking) distinguishes content identity, piece/target and impression/interaction. We borrow the separation, not their tracking implementation: a requested evidence page can be related to an article without becoming another article event or proof of use. A registry's declared identity remains scoped to its version; mutable titles must not create new parents.

The [WHATWG URL pathname contract](https://url.spec.whatwg.org/#dom-url-pathname) concerns a serialized path. URL parsing can transform dot segments; query and fragment are separate. Our exact mapping must reject noncanonical registry paths and keep unmatched event variants visible. Explicit aliases are an operator assertion, not something the parser independently authenticates.

## What this changes, and what remains

We can now compare access to an article with access to its supporting material without inventing a second article view or joining mutable titles into duplicate parents. Evidence-page access is a supported reach outcome. It still cannot establish a reader’s purpose, task completion or usefulness.

No fresh traffic, human label, native-provider oracle, privacy decision, purpose or demand result is claimed.
