## Rejected or narrowed claims

- “Server-side AI reporting is unique to this library”: contradicted by Matomo/PostHog, with server-log paths elsewhere.
- “Retaining several detection reasons is unique”: contradicted by Rybbit's documented matching layers.
- “An AI dashboard proves agents used or cited the content”: collection, retrieval, answer use, referral and conversion need separate evidence.
- “More bots rejected means higher accuracy”: requires independent labels and a declared cost for false positives, false negatives and abstention.
- “A vendor's bot purpose identifies this request's actual task”: a declared category does not authenticate a specific prompt or behavior.
- “All products were installed and compared”: only documentation/source were rechecked here; the owned installation/runtime checks belong to prior acceptance.
- “Our protocol work is complete”: five public-key algorithms are implemented; HMAC distribution, transport context, digest validation and nonce enforcement retain explicit scope boundaries.
- “The migration was delivered in production”: new source activated and an empty-selection cache control passed, while existing-facts mutation was rejected by automatic approval review and did not run.
