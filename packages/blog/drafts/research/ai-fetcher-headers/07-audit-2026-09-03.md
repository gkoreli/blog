## Verdict

The package is not yet reliable as the durable reference page it claims to be. Its central observations remain valuable and mostly well supported—especially the two eight-request anonymous Grok bursts, the vendor-list matches, the Perplexity/Copilot answer-time absences, and DuckAssistBot's signed requests—but blocker-class problems cross the evidence boundary: a prompt that was never used is printed as verbatim method, relied-on raw requests are not in the advertised dataset, Grok's anonymous behavior is generalized to signed-in Grok, Codex's late request is assigned to an unnamed internal pipeline as fact, and vendor-wide signing/non-signing claims contradict the documentation artifact. Correct those, re-synchronize the method/data/worklist/changelog, then address the numerical, quotation, editorial, and task-state issues before treating post 023 as publication-safe.
