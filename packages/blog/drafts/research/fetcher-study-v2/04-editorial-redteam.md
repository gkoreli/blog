1. **An external guide used the capture.** Identify the publication, exact technical use and direct destination. Explain that original experiments produced something another author could use.
2. **The original inference needed hardening.** Show the historical proof and its actual coverage. Preserve the distinction between a dated capture, a later replay and a new experiment.
3. **What the completed work made better.** Select the improvements consequential to this study: coverage evidence, separate declared role/signing status, trace fidelity, unknown/failure states and reproducible receipts. Give the reader an understandable before/after example rather than a list of release hashes.
4. **A fresh falsifiable question.** Present the newly frozen local test, alternatives, inputs, results and any resulting implementation change. Do not relabel the existing 20-case replay, 52-proof cohort or 13 native tracing controls as new trials.
5. **A stronger reference others can reuse.** Show the resulting artifact/test or decision table. Link back to the stable original URL and reproducible evidence. Report external reuse positively and keep aggregate traffic in its own dated unit and method.

This scope can include the strongest recent growth and engineering results without retelling every analytics incident.
