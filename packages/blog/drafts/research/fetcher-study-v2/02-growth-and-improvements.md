No inference about whether a person or agent found the study is needed to recognize the outcome.

## What the retained windows do not support

The four complete Browser-selected weeks have 1,477 included events: 971 canonical article,114 evidence-page and392 site events. The last overall comparison is312→691; removing fixed unexplained R1 descriptively yields308→303. At article-resource level the latest comparison outside R1 is173→180; evidence pages are37→22. These are supported descriptive results, not an adjusted human-growth estimate. Of the1,477 events,1,245 have both method stamps null. Equal nulls do not prove stable measurement.

The97-row and397-row all-kind slices have adjacent but unequal partial-day windows, a profile change, and23 raw Browser records overlapping the complete-day capture. Do not sum them into a larger audience or present97→397 as growth. The proposed latest suffix never ran: D1 metadata failed authorization, and no production SELECT followed. This inventory does not retry that path.

An actual [Perplexity answer trial](../discovery-experiment-02/14-public-answer-fidelity-results.md) supplies a useful separate result: its neutral answer cites Arcjet and makes three supported general distinctions, while omitting three required capture-specific details. One completed answer, one sign-in-blocked condition and one unsubmitted condition remain separate. Source appearance, answer citation and successful task completion are each observable; the presence of the Arcjet citation does not prove that answer consumed our linked passage. Cloudless's independently published DuckAssistBot observation is a corroborating source lead, not an established citation to our study.

## Editorial recommendation

Make the follow-up a new, evidence-led explanation of what the original investigation made possible: original request captures became reusable source material; subsequent tests exposed where signature and audience claims needed more precision; tools now preserve those distinctions for later operators. Arcjet's citation is a substantive outcome of that work, not a footnote or merely a correction prospect.

Keep the September original as the stable dated reference. Its v2 layer can add a concise dated route to the follow-up, fresh trial results in a separate table only when actually run, and an explicit distinction among declaration, network, covered proof and unknown trigger. Preserve the original raw captures, historical counts, URL, publication date and frozen footprint. Article030 already explains the broad analytics build; avoid reproducing its whole operational narrative here.

Useful article structure: the external technical reuse; the precise promise of a verified request; a new experiment whose result changes a reader decision; the retained-log lesson and current repair; the positive discovery/evidence-access results; the reusable method and remaining question. Present each measurement beside its actual date and conditions. No independent operator installation, current vendor retest, fresh traffic growth rate, managed-provider oracle or human/device accuracy result is established by this inventory.
