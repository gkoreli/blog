**Current line 108:**

> `## “AI Reads” could not see a direct AI read`

### 2. Remove the awkward self-observation sentence

**Current line 151:**

> `The observation path worked. At this traffic level, the first thing it mostly observed was the attempt to observe it.`

`first thing`, `mostly`, and three forms of `observe` fight each other.

**Change direction:**

> `The path worked, but at this traffic level the investigation mostly measured its own requests.`

Keep the finding, not the manufactured punchline.

### 3. Replace measurement jargon

**Current line 153:**

> `It does establish that automatic consumption had a poor observed base rate in that sample.`

**Change to:**

> `It does establish that automatic requests were rare in that sample.`

The simpler sentence is more precise: the study measured requests, not consumption.

### 4. Cut the repeated four-line verdict

Lines 170–176 repeat the opening answer and the `Should you add llms.txt?` table.

The stage table already carries the distinction. Repeating the verdict makes the article feel assembled.

### 5. Make two headings search-legible instead of slogan-shaped

**Current line 246:**
