## Primary references

[OpenTelemetry's log data model](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/otel/logs/data-model/) distinguishes source event time from collection observation time, allowing source time to be unknown. Its [HTTP span conventions](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/http/http-spans/#http-client-span-duration) allow client spans to end after headers, with body inclusion varying. These are useful semantic distinctions, not a certification of our timestamps or an OTel integration. We borrow the distinction and add no dependency. The saved controller code, rather than the names of its fields, determines what our clocks mean.
