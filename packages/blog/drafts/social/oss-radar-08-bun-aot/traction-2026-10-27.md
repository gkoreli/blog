# Bun AOT launch: little X exposure, killed HN submission

Checked October 27, 2026 Pacific. The final platform checks were around **October 28, 02:40 UTC / October 27, 7:40 PM PDT**, about **9 hours 38 minutes** after posting. This supersedes the launch receipt's implication that the signed-in HN view established ordinary public availability.

**No meaningful traction is demonstrated. X recorded few impressions; HN killed the submission. One browser-shaped request reporting a `t.co` referrer is the only candidate article visit in the site's Browser category.** It is not proof of a human reader or of a particular post click.

There are two relevant process failures:

1. HN's current [submission rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) prohibit generated text and automated posting, and discourage primarily promotional use. This launch was submitted through browser automation with collaboratively generated copy. The 12-story history consists of the author's blog and GitHub project. These are relevant rule conflicts/risk factors, not proof of the actual trigger. The earlier seven dead stories predate this launch, so this one submission cannot explain the whole pattern.
2. The launch verification checked only signed-in owner pages. It should not have reported ordinary public availability without an anonymous/API check. The missing optional text was also interpreted too casually: [child item 50262192](https://hacker-news.firebaseio.com/v0/item/50262192.json) is a comment with the same submission timestamp and is marked dead and deleted. Its text is unavailable publicly. No separate comment action was issued in this session; the record does not establish who or what deleted the child.

## Decision and remaining uncertainty

- **Keep:** the article's supported measurements and this frozen evidence. No article correction is justified by these reach figures.
- **Change next time:** check destination rules before cross-posting, use an owner-operated HN submission path consistent with its rules, and verify public status independently of the owner view. Check native quote/reply context rather than infer type solely from a leading `@`.
- **Evidence:** X owner analytics and export, ten visually classified originals, public HN API/history/anonymous responses, the exact short-link destination, and two narrowly filtered site reports before and after the tool delay.
- **Confidence and confounders:** high confidence in little measured X exposure and HN's dead state; no confirmed human readership; unknown X ranking cause and unknown HN moderation trigger. Site request labels, self-views, unmatched post ages, export lag and unequal measurement methods remain material limits.
- **Article correction needed:** no.
- **Shared-skill change needed:** yes, for destination rules, independent visibility checks and quote/reply classification.

The next useful HN action is for the owner to ask moderators to clarify the account/submission status, citing these item IDs. No moderator message, new post, deletion, repost, comment, vote solicitation or monitoring automation was performed for this investigation. Any native-proof X experiment would be a proposed new test requiring a new publication request; it is not a proven remedy.
