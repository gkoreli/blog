## What the evidence does and does not prove

The strongest evidence here is **an editor already selecting this subject**, **a community discussing comparable measurements**, or **a destination explicitly accepting submissions**. Those establish fit or a route. They do not establish expected clicks, readership, acceptance, or conversion. None of the new tactics is proven to work for this account yet.

The earlier [traction report](traction-2026-10-27.md) established little X exposure and a hidden HN submission. It did not establish that readers rejected the article. A fresh check around 6:30 PM Pacific on October 28 showed:

- RAM reply: 8 visible views; Zod reply: 10 visible views, about 8¾ hours after publication. Neither displayed a reply or social reaction. Views can include the author and repeats; these are not counts of people.
- The HN moderator email thread contained only our sent message. No response in that thread at this check; this is not an inbox-wide assertion.
- No site analytics query was made for this research. The earlier Browser observation is not refreshed or promoted to a confirmed reader.

Private DOM captures are outside Git in the task's temporary promotion-evidence folder. These are small observations, not a matched experiment.

- **HN repost:** the visibility failure remains unresolved; no moderator reply in the checked thread. Do not delete/repost or recruit votes. [HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) require human-written submissions and prohibit automated posting.
- **r/typescript copy-paste:** its [rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/typescript/about/rules.json) explicitly reject ChatGPT posts/comments and excessive self-promotion. Do not prepare an automated publishing path there.

- **Lobsters as a new account:** its [guidelines](https://lobste.rs/about) prohibit self-promotion for the first 70 days and give a less-than-one-quarter rule of thumb afterward. Do not ask another user to submit as a workaround.
- **Console newsletter pitch:** its [selection criteria](https://console.dev/selection-criteria) select developer tools; this is an article. Not a good use of this pitch.
- **Bytes/TypeScript Weekly mass pitches:** the sites exist, but this pass did not verify a current editorial submission route and comparable outcome. Do not guess an address or buy a placement just to create activity.
- **Another CommonJS bug issue:** the existing [issue 47044](https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/issues/47044) already has a linked focused reproduction. A link dump in an issue tracker is not useful promotion.
- **More internal links:** the repository's Bun 1.4 article already links forward to this article. That work is complete; do not present it as a fresh growth tactic. No production activation was rechecked here.

## What to measure and when to stop

After each authorized action, verify the exact published destination and ordinary public visibility, then save a timestamped receipt. Inspect near 24 hours and seven days when practical; **this plan schedules nothing**. For pitches, selection/rejection or a substantive reply is the first useful signal. For community posts, record real questions, corrections, reproductions and reports of use. Record delivery, platform clicks and site requests separately. Avoid repeated broad production queries.

If people see the post but misunderstand the result, fix that explanation. If hardly anyone sees it, test another checked destination before rewriting the article. If a useful conversation forms, answer it with evidence and incorporate corrections. Do not convert one small result into a claim about an algorithm.
